## 🔥 Warm-up: Would you buy it?
Look at 3 real product cards. Would you buy? What made you trust it, or not?

## 💡 Big idea: a great product card
1. 🖼️ **Honest picture** (label AI pictures)
2. 🏷️ **Clear name**
3. 💲 **Price**, no surprises
4. ✨ **2 or 3 benefits**
5. 🎨 **Options** (size, color)
6. 🛒 **Button**

**Price math:** cost (make + pack + ship) + profit = price.
Example: cost $9, price $20 → profit $11.

```try
Check my math. My bottle costs $6 to make, $1 to pack and $2 to ship. I want to sell it for $20. What is my profit? Is $20 a fair price compared to similar steel water bottles?
```
