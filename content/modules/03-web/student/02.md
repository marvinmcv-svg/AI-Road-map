## 🔥 Warm-up: 5-second test
Look at 3 real landing pages for 5 seconds each. What do they sell? What do they want you to do?

## 💡 Big idea: the landing page recipe
| Part | Visitor thinks… |
|---|---|
| 1. 🦸 **Hero** | "What is this?" |
| 2. ✨ **Reasons** | "Why should I care?" |
| 3. 🪜 **How it works** | "Is it easy?" |
| 4. ⭐ **Proof** | "Can I trust you?" |
| 5. 👉 **Call to action** | "I want it!" |
| 6. 📇 **Footer** | "Who are you?" |

**One page, one goal.** Every part points to the same button.
**Phone first.** Most people will see it on a phone.

## 🛠️ Your build: Wireframe
- [ ] Write your page's ONE goal
- [ ] Draw all 6 parts on the phone sheet, top to bottom
- [ ] Mark where your logo, video and product pictures go
- [ ] Draw the laptop version
