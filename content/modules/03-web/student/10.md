## 🔥 Warm-up: Guess the trouble
Where will a new visitor get stuck on your site? Write your guess. Check it later!

## 💡 Big idea: the User Test Script
1. 👋 "We're testing the site, not you."
2. 💭 "Please think out loud."
3. ✅ Give 3 tasks, one at a time:
   - "What does this company sell?"
   - "Find a product and order it."
   - "Find out how to return a product."
4. 🤐 **Don't help!** Just watch, time it and take notes.
5. 📝 Short survey at the end.

Stuck = something to fix!

```try
Here are my user test notes and survey answers: [paste them]. Sort the feedback into themes. What are the 3 biggest problems, and what fix would you try for each?
```

## 🛠️ Your build
- [ ] Make a 4-question survey in Google Forms
- [ ] Test with 2 or 3 people
- [ ] For each task, write: done? how long? where stuck?
- [ ] Sort the feedback with Gemini, then decide yourself
- [ ] Make your top 3 fixes and publish again
