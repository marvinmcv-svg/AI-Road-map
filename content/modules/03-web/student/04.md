## 🔥 Warm-up: Plan check
Name the 6 landing page parts. Read your hero out loud. Still clear?

## 💡 Big idea: Google Sites in 6 steps
1. ➕ **New site**
2. 🎨 **Theme** — your brand colors (with codes) and fonts
3. 🧩 **Insert** — text, pictures, buttons, side-by-side boxes, your video from Drive
4. 🖼️ **Banner** — a wide Gemini picture with empty space for words
5. 📱 **Preview** — check the phone view!
6. 🚀 **Publish** — pick your web address

```try
Flat icon of a snowflake in teal #1CB0F6, thick round lines, white background, no words, square 1:1.
```

## 🛠️ Your build
- [ ] Set the theme: brand colors, fonts and logo
- [ ] Hero: banner, headline, one line, big button
- [ ] Reasons: 3 boxes with matching Gemini icons
- [ ] How it works: 3 steps + your video
- [ ] Proof and call to action
- [ ] Footer: brand email and "about us"
- [ ] Check the phone preview and fix anything squished
- [ ] Publish!
