## 🔥 Warm-up: Checkout fears
Have you ever stopped buying something at checkout? Why?

## 💡 Big idea: the checkout path
**Product → Cart → Details → Pay → Thank you.** Every extra step loses buyers.

**Two ways to take orders:**
- 📝 **Order form** (Google Forms) — no money, great for pre-orders
- 💳 **Payment link** (Stripe, **test mode**) — pretend money, a real checkout feel

**Only ask for what you need:** name, email, product, how many, address if you ship.
**Never** ask for card numbers in a form.
