## 🔥 Warm-up: Peek behind the page
Open a website. Right-click → **View page source**. Find the page title in the code!
📣 **New quest:** your company needs a home online!

## 💡 Big idea
When you type a **web address**, your **browser** asks the internet for the page. A computer (the **host**) sends back the files. Your browser draws the page.

| Code | Job | Like a house… |
|---|---|---|
| **HTML** | What's on the page | the walls and rooms |
| **CSS** | How it looks | the paint and furniture |
| **JavaScript** | What happens when you click | the lights and doorbell |

**You don't have to write the code.** Claude writes it and shows it as an **artifact** (a live preview). You're the boss!

**Web Prompt Recipe:** What it is · Who it's for · Sections in order · Look · Rules

```try
Make a one-page website for "Sip Squad", a water bottle that never leaks, for college students who walk a lot. Sections in order: a big top part with a headline and an "Order now" button, 3 reasons to buy with icons, and a footer. Look: teal #1CB0F6 and white, round friendly font. Rules: works on phones, big buttons, short words.
```
