## 💡 Big idea: the Trust Checklist
1. 🙅 **No tricks** — no fake timers, hidden fees, pre-ticked boxes or guilt buttons
2. 📄 **Privacy note** — what you collect, why, who sees it, how long you keep it
3. 📦 **Store rules** — shipping, returns, refunds, contact
4. 📧 **Real contact** — a brand email someone reads
5. 🔐 **Locked accounts** — strong password + 2-step login
6. 🏷️ **AI labels** on AI pictures

**Fake store red flags:** too-good-to-be-true prices · no contact info · weird web address · "pay now!" pressure · gift cards only
