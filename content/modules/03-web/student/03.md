## 🔥 Warm-up: Clear or confusing?
Read 5 real headlines. Do you know what each one sells? Fix the most confusing one!

## 💡 Big idea: the 4 copy rules
1. 💡 **Clear beats clever.** "Water bottles that never leak" beats "Hydration, reimagined."
2. 😊 **Benefits beat features.** Feature: "double steel walls." Benefit: "ice-cold water all day."
3. 🔘 **Buttons say what happens.** "Order my bottle" beats "Submit".
4. ✅ **Only true claims.** No fake reviews. No fake "only 2 left!".

**Gemini tip:** ask for 10 options. Then YOU choose and change.

```try
Give me 10 short headlines for a landing page for a water bottle that never leaks, for college students who walk a lot. Clear, not clever. 10 words or less each.
```

## 🛠️ Your build: Page Copy
- [ ] Hero: headline, one line under it, button words
- [ ] Reasons: 3 features turned into benefits
- [ ] How it works: 3 short steps
- [ ] Proof: 2 honest trust lines (no fake reviews)
- [ ] Call to action: price + button
- [ ] Footer: brand email + short "about us"
- [ ] Ask Gemini to point out anything untrue or hard to read, then fix it

## ⚔️ Challenge: Feature to Benefit
Draw a feature card. Turn it into a benefit in 30 seconds!

```group
Your house competes. The class votes for the best benefit.
```

```solo
**You vs. your teacher.** Same card, both write a benefit. Pick each other's best!
```

## 🎟️ Exit ticket
1. What's the difference between a feature and a benefit? Give an example.
2. Rewrite this button: "Submit".
3. Why is a fake review a problem, even a "small" one?

## 🛡️ Stay safe
Every claim must be true. If you can't prove it, don't say it.
