## 🔥 Warm-up: Word Lightning Round
Your teacher shows a word. Say what it means and give an example. Fast!

## 💡 What "Pro" looks like
Look at the rubric with your teacher, one row at a time.

## 🛠️ Your build: Launch checklist
- [ ] Hero says what you sell in 5 seconds, with one big button
- [ ] All 6 landing page parts are there
- [ ] Brand colors, logo and launch video are on the page
- [ ] Your Canvas section works on phone and computer
- [ ] Shop has 3+ honest product cards with prices
- [ ] A test order goes all the way to "thank you"
- [ ] Works for Everyone checklist passes
- [ ] Trust checklist passes
- [ ] Top fixes from user testing are done
- [ ] Score yourself with the rubric and fix your lowest row

## ⚔️ Challenge: Launch Day
A 3-minute live walkthrough:
> "This is ___. It's for ___. Our one goal is ___. Testers struggled with ___, so I changed ___. The AI made ___. I made ___."

Then place a test order live!

```group
Every guest places a test order and gives one ⭐ strength and one ❓ question.
```

```solo
Count down "3, 2, 1, launch!" and share your link with your teacher and a guest. Watch their test orders arrive!
```

## 🎟️ Exit ticket
1. What part of your site are you most proud of? Why?
2. What did user testing teach you?
3. What would you add with one more lesson?

## 🛡️ Stay safe
Only share your site if you want to. Keep checkout in test mode.
