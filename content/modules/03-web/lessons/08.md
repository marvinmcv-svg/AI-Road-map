## Lesson objectives
By the end, the student can…
- Explain the **checkout path**: cart → details → pay → thank you.
- Build an order form (or a test payment link) and connect it to their product buttons.
- Collect only the info they really need, and keep it safe.
- Write a clear order confirmation message.

## Words for today
- **Checkout** — the steps to buy something online.
- **Order form** — a form where buyers say what they want and where to send it.
- **Payment link** — a link that takes buyers to a safe page to pay.
- **Test mode** — a practice mode with pretend money and pretend cards.
- **Personal data** — info about a person: name, email, address, phone.
- **Confirmation** — the "thank you, here's your order" message.

## 1. Warm-up — Checkout fears (10 min · 1-on-1: 5 min)
Ask: "Have you ever stopped buying something at checkout? Why?" (surprise fees, too many boxes to fill, looked unsafe, forced to make an account).

```solo
The student tells a real story of a checkout that went wrong (or right). Write down the "fears". You'll avoid them today.
```

## 2. Mini-lesson — The checkout path (15 min · 1-on-1: 10 min)
Show **Visual 1**: **Product → Cart → Details → Pay → Thank you.** Every extra step loses buyers.

**Two ways to take orders in this course:**
| | Order form (Google Forms) | Payment link (Stripe, test mode) |
|---|---|---|
| Money | No money. You'd collect payment later. | Pretend money now; real money only if you switch it on for real. |
| Best for | Pre-orders, waitlists, local sales | Practicing a real online checkout |
| Setup | 10 minutes | 20 minutes |

**The privacy rule: "Only what you need."** (**Visual 2**)
- Need: name, email, what they want, how many. Address only if you ship.
- Don't need: birthday, phone (unless for delivery), anything else.
- Never ask for card numbers in a Google Form. Card details only go through a real payment page (like Stripe).
- Keep the answers sheet private. Don't share it.

**Thank-you message:** say what they ordered, what happens next, when, and how to contact you.

## 3. Hands-on build — Take your first order (35 min · 1-on-1: 25 min)
1. Make an **order form** in Google Forms: name, email, product (dropdown), color, how many, shipping address (if needed), and a checkbox "I understand this is a pre-order."
2. Ask Gemini to write the form intro and confirmation message in your brand voice. Check it's true.
3. Link each product button on your Shop page to the form. Republish.
4. **Level-up (optional):** in Stripe **test mode**, make a Payment Link for one product and link its button. Pay with a test card.
5. Place a test order yourself. Check it lands in the answers sheet.

**Checkpoint 1 (+20 XP):** order form live and linked from the Shop page.
**Checkpoint 2 (+20 XP):** test order placed, confirmation message works, form asks only for what's needed.

```group
Pairs place test orders on each other's stores.
```

```solo
You place a test order on their store while they watch the answers sheet fill in. Then they place one on yours. Talk about any step that felt slow.
```

> 🛡️ **Data moment:** the order sheet has real people's info if anyone orders. Keep it private, don't use it for anything else, and delete it when you're done.

## 4. Game / challenge — Checkout Speedrun (15 min · 1-on-1: 10 min)
Time how long it takes to order from your store, from the home page to "thank you". Then cut one step or one box and try again.

```group
Fastest checkout (that still asks for what's needed): +15 XP. Everyone who cuts a step: +5.
```

```solo
**Student vs. Bolt.** You both speedrun each other's stores. Whoever's store is faster to buy from wins. +15 XP for playing, +5 for cutting a step.
```

## 5. Share-out — Click to thank you (10 min · 1-on-1: 5 min)
Show the whole path live, from the hero button to the thank-you message.

```solo
Record a quick screen video of the student's checkout path. It's great for their portfolio.
```

## 6. Exit ticket (5 min)
1. Name the steps of a checkout.
2. What info should your order form NOT ask for?
3. What is test mode for?

```solo
Ask out loud. Look at their form together and remove any question they don't need.
```

## Differentiation
- **Extra help:** use the order form only. Start from a form template with the questions already there.
- **Extra challenge:** set up the Stripe test Payment Link with 2 options (color) and a thank-you redirect back to your site. Or turn on an email that goes out by itself when someone orders.
- **1-on-1 tip:** if the student might really sell something one day, walk through what changes when you leave test mode (taxes, fees, real refunds). Keep it in test mode in class.
