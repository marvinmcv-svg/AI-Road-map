## Lesson objectives
By the end, the student can…
- Name the 6 parts of a good **product card**.
- Set a price using **cost**, **profit** and a quick look at other sellers.
- Build a Shop page with 3 or more honest product cards.
- Write simple, true shipping and return rules.

## Words for today
- **Product card** — a box for one product: picture, name, price, short words, button.
- **Cost** — what it costs you to make and send the product.
- **Price** — what the buyer pays.
- **Profit** — price minus cost. The money you keep.
- **Shipping** — how the product gets to the buyer, and what it costs.
- **Returns** — the rules for sending a product back.

## 1. Warm-up — Would you buy it? (10 min · 1-on-1: 5 min)
```teacher
Today your website becomes a store. People need to trust a store before they buy. What makes YOU trust one?
```

Show 3 real product cards. For each: would you buy it? What made you trust it, or not?

```solo
The student shows you something they bought online and tells you what on the product page made them click "buy".
```

## 2. Mini-lesson — Cards and prices (15 min · 1-on-1: 10 min)
**A great product card** (**Visual 1**):
1. 🖼️ **Honest picture** — shows the real product (label AI pictures, and never show it bigger or better than it is)
2. 🏷️ **Clear name** — "Sip Bottle, 750 ml, Teal"
3. 💲 **Price** — easy to find, no surprise costs
4. ✨ **2 or 3 benefits** — short lines
5. 🎨 **Options** — size, color
6. 🛒 **Button** — "Add to cart" or "Order this one"

**Price math** (**Visual 2**):
- **Cost** = making it + packing + shipping (+ app fees)
- **Price** = cost + profit
- Example: cost $9, price $20 → profit $11
- Then look at 3 similar products online. Are you way higher or lower? Why?

**Shipping and returns:** say how long it takes, what it costs, and how to send it back. Only promise what you can really do.

## 3. Hands-on build — Build your Shop page (35 min · 1-on-1: 25 min)
1. **Pricing sheet:** fill in costs and pick a price for each product. Ask Claude to check your math.
2. **Pictures:** make product pictures in Gemini (same style, same light, plain background). Add "AI picture" in the alt text or a small label if it's not a real photo.
3. **Shop page:** ask Claude Code: "Add a Shop page with a card for each of these products: [names, prices, benefits, picture files]. Add an 'Add to cart' counter in the menu. Match our brand." Check it in the preview.
4. Add a short **Shipping & Returns** section.
5. Link the hero button on your home page to the Shop page. Deploy again.

**Checkpoint 1 (+20 XP):** pricing sheet done, each product has a profit.
**Checkpoint 2 (+20 XP):** Shop page live with 3+ cards and shipping/returns rules.

```group
Pairs check each other's math before building.
```

```solo
Play the buyer. Ask hard questions: "Why does it cost that much?" "How fast does it get to me?" "What if it breaks?" Every answer should be on the page.
```

> 🛡️ **Honest store moment:** the picture, the price and the promise must match what the buyer really gets. No hidden fees at the end.

## 4. Game / challenge — Card Judge (15 min · 1-on-1: 10 min)
Rate product cards (yours and real ones) on the 6 parts. 1 point per part. Fix one weak part on your own card.

```group
Houses rate each other's cards. Highest score: +15 XP. Everyone who fixes a part: +5.
```

```solo
**Student vs. Bolt.** You each make one product card for a silly product (a "self-tying shoelace") in 5 minutes with Claude. Rate each other. +15 XP for playing, +5 for the higher score.
```

## 5. Share-out — Shop tour (10 min · 1-on-1: 5 min)
Walk through your shop. Which product would you buy first? Why?

```solo
The student gives you a 60-second shop tour, like a store clerk. Ask one buyer question.
```

## 6. Exit ticket (5 min)
1. Name 4 parts of a product card.
2. If it costs $8 to make and send, and the price is $18, what's the profit?
3. Why must the picture match the real product?

```solo
Ask out loud. Check #2 together on the pricing sheet.
```

## Differentiation
- **Extra help:** 3 products with the same shape, only different colors. Use the pricing sheet with costs already filled in.
- **Extra challenge:** add a bundle ("2 bottles for $35") and work out if it still makes a profit. Or add a "sold out" state for one product.
- **1-on-1 tip:** if the student has a real side hustle or hobby they could sell, use it! Real numbers make this lesson come alive.
