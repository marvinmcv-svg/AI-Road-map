## Lesson objectives
By the end, the student can…
- Build every section of their wireframe in Google Sites.
- Use their brand theme: colors, fonts, logo.
- Add pictures and their launch video, then **publish** the site.

## Words for today
- **Theme** — the colors and fonts a whole site uses.
- **Section (block)** — one part of a page, like the hero or the reasons.
- **Banner** — a wide picture across the top.
- **Embed** — putting something from another place (like a video) inside your page.
- **Publish** — making your site live, with a web address anyone can open.

## 1. Warm-up — Plan check (10 min · 1-on-1: 5 min)
Recall: name the 6 landing page parts. Then read your hero copy out loud. Is it still clear? Any last fix?

```solo
The student reads the hero; you say what you think it sells. If you're wrong, fix the copy before building.
```

## 2. Mini-lesson — Google Sites in 10 minutes (10 min · 1-on-1: 7 min)
Live demo (**Visual 1**):
1. **New site** → pick a simple layout.
2. **Themes** → choose one, then set your brand colors (custom color codes) and fonts.
3. **Insert** → text boxes, images, buttons, layouts (side-by-side boxes), Drive (for your video), YouTube.
4. **Header banner** → a wide picture. Make one in Gemini at 16:9 with empty space for words.
5. **Preview** → check the phone view!
6. **Publish** → pick a web address name.

**Pictures tip:** for icons and banners, ask Gemini: "flat icon of a snowflake in teal #1CB0F6, white background, no words, 1:1." Same style words for every icon so they match.

## 3. Hands-on build — Build and publish (45 min · 1-on-1: 31 min)
Follow your wireframe, top to bottom:
1. Set the theme: brand colors and fonts. Add your logo.
2. **Hero:** banner, headline, one line, big button.
3. **Reasons:** 3 boxes side by side, each with a Gemini icon, a title and one line.
4. **How it works:** 3 steps + your explainer or launch video (Insert → Drive).
5. **Proof** and **call to action** (button again + price).
6. **Footer:** brand email, social links, a short "about us".
7. Check the **phone preview**. Fix anything squished.
8. **Publish.**

**Checkpoint 1 (+20 XP):** theme set + hero and reasons built.
**Checkpoint 2 (+20 XP):** all sections built, video in, site published.

Buttons can point to "#" for now. Next lessons add the store and order form.

```group
Walk around and ask: "Which part of your wireframe is this?"
```

```solo
Let the student drive. You keep the wireframe and copy in your hands and read out the next section. Celebrate the publish click!
```

> 🛡️ **Sharing moment:** check who can see your site in the Publish settings. Use your brand email, never your personal phone or home address.

## 4. Game / challenge — Plan vs. Page (10 min · 1-on-1: 7 min)
Hold the wireframe next to the live page. Find everything that got lost or changed. 1 point per thing you find, 1 more for fixing it.

```group
Pairs check each other's site. Most fixes: +15 XP. Everyone: +5.
```

```solo
**Student vs. Bolt.** You check their site; they check a demo site you made with 4 hidden mistakes (a missing button, a broken video, a wrong color, a typo). +15 XP for playing, +5 for finding all 4.
```

## 5. Share-out — Launch link (10 min · 1-on-1: 5 min)
Open the published site on a phone. Feedback: *I like… / I wonder… / Try…*

```solo
The student sends you the link, and you open it on your phone while they watch. Say your first thought out loud.
```

## 6. Exit ticket (5 min)
1. What's a theme?
2. How did you put your video on the page?
3. What's one thing you'll improve next lesson?

```solo
Ask out loud. Add #3 to their fix list.
```

## Differentiation
- **Extra help:** start from a ready-made Google Sites template and just swap in their words, colors and pictures. Hero + reasons + call to action is enough today.
- **Extra challenge:** add a second page ("About us" or "FAQ") and a menu. Make the FAQ with Gemini, then check every answer is true.
- **1-on-1 tip:** take a phone photo of the wireframe and keep it open next to Sites. Ticking off boxes feels great.
