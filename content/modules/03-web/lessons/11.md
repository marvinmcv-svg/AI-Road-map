## Lesson objectives
By the end, the student can…
- Finish a live landing page + store that works on phones and computers.
- Score their own site with the rubric and fix the weakest part.
- Give a live walkthrough from the first click to a test order, explaining their choices.

## Words for today
All the Module 3 words. Most of all: landing page, hero, call to action, product card, checkout, alt text, privacy note, user test.

---

## 1. Warm-up — Word lightning round (5 min)
Show a word. Say what it means and give an example. 10 words, fast.

```group
First house to answer scores.
```

```solo
Time it: how many words in 2 minutes? Try to beat your Module 2 score.
```

## 2. Mini-lesson — What "Pro" looks like (5 min)
Go through the rubric's **4 · Pro** column, one row at a time. Show a strong example (your own demo site is great).

## 3. Hands-on build — Launch checklist (35 min · 1-on-1: 25 min)
- [ ] Hero says what you sell in 5 seconds, with one big button
- [ ] All 6 landing page parts are there
- [ ] Brand colors, logo and launch video are on the page
- [ ] Custom Canvas section works on phone and computer
- [ ] Shop: 3+ honest product cards with prices
- [ ] Checkout works: a test order goes from click to thank you
- [ ] Works for Everyone checklist passes (phone, alt text, contrast, headings, link words, captions)
- [ ] Trust checklist passes (no tricks, privacy note, store rules, real contact, 2-step login, AI labels)
- [ ] Top fixes from user testing are done

Score yourself with the rubric. Write one sentence of proof for each row. Last 10 minutes: fix the lowest row. Republish.
**Checkpoint (+20 XP):** checklist done + self-score written.

```solo
Let the student self-score first, then compare with your score. Where you disagree, open the site and look together.
```

## 4. Game / challenge — Launch Day (30 min · 1-on-1: 15 min)
A live walkthrough on the big screen (3 minutes):
- "This is ___. It's for ___. Our one goal is ___."
- Show the hero, scroll through the page, play 5 seconds of the video, use the custom section.
- Place a test order live, all the way to "thank you".
- "The thing users struggled with was ___, so I changed ___."
- "The AI made ___. I made ___."

Guests act as "first customers": each one places a test order on their own phone and says one ⭐ strength and one ❓ question.

**Boss XP:** rubric total (4 rows × 1 to 4 points) × 6 = up to 96. Round up to 100 for a perfect score. **🌐 Web Smith** badge for 3 or more on every row.

```group
Awards: "Best Hero", "Smoothest Checkout", "Most Trustworthy Store" (+15 house XP each).
```

```solo
**Grand opening.** Make it feel real: count down "3, 2, 1, launch!", then share the link with the guests at the same moment. Watch the test orders arrive in the sheet together. Award "Best in Show" to their best part (+15 XP).
```

## 5. Share-out — Launch party (10 min · 1-on-1: 5 min)
Celebrate! Then tease the next quest: *"Your store is open… and the orders, emails and questions are piling up. Time to build AI helpers that do the busy work for you."*

```solo
Help the student share their site link with anyone they like (only if they want to). Ask: "What part are you proudest of?"
```

## 6. Exit ticket (5 min)
1. What part of your site are you most proud of? Why?
2. What did user testing teach you?
3. What would you add with one more lesson?

```solo
Ask out loud. Save the answers for a progress note.
```

## Differentiation
- **Extra help:** a landing page + 3-product shop with an order form is enough for the badge at level 3. Use the checklist step by step.
- **Extra challenge:** connect a custom web address (domain) to the Google Site. Or add a simple "Join our email list" form and write the first welcome email with Gemini.
- **1-on-1 tip:** real "first customers" make this a day to remember. Even one guest placing a test order on their own phone feels big.
