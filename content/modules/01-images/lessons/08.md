## Lesson objectives
By the end, the student can…
- Keep a character the same in many pictures. Use a **character sheet**, follow-up asks and a fixed **style line**.
- Spot and fix common **AI glitches**.
- Talk about who should own AI art, with reasons for different sides.

## Words for today
- **Character sheet** — the exact words that describe your character. Copy them every time.
- **Style guide** — the rules for how the brand looks (colors, fonts, do's and don'ts).
- **Same every time (consistency)** — the character looks like the same one in every picture.
- **Glitch** — a weird AI mistake, like 6 fingers or melted letters.
- **Copyright** — who owns a picture, song or story, and who can use it.

## 1. Warm-up — Recall Raid (all mixed up) (10 min · 1-on-1: 5 min)
1. What does image AI start from?
2. Which camera shot makes something look big and strong?
3. What's the difference between "swap one thing" and "expand"?
4. What's an icon + name logo?
5. Why do we add labels to diagrams ourselves?

```solo
Rapid fire, out loud. Any miss? Make a flashcard for it in their deck from last lesson.
```

## 2. Mini-lesson — The "same character" problem (15 min · 1-on-1: 10 min)
Show the **bad example**: "a cute robot mascot" in 3 scenes → three different robots. Why? Every picture starts from new random static, so small details change.
Show the **good example** with a **character sheet** (**Visual 1**):
- **Fixed description** (copy and paste the same words every time): *"Bolt, a small round teal robot with one big yellow eye, orange antenna, short stubby legs, white belly panel with a leaf logo."*
- **Fixed style line:** *"flat cartoon, thick outlines, soft shadows, brand colors teal, lime and white."*
- **Stay in the same Gemini chat:** make the first good picture. Then ask: "Same character, now skateboarding." Gemini looks back at the picture it already made.
- **Turnaround sheet:** ask for front, side and back views on one page.

```solo
Show the bad example first and ask the student "What went wrong?" before you show the fix. Let them discover it.
```

## 3. Hands-on build — Mascot & Style Guide (35 min · 1-on-1: 25 min)
1. Write the mascot's **fixed description** + **style line** (5 min).
2. Make the mascot in **Gemini**. Pick the best one. → **Checkpoint 1 (+20 XP).**
3. In the same chat, make the mascot in 3 scenes ("Same character, now…"). Score each one 1 to 5 for "same character?".
4. Fix glitches: make it again, or ask for a fix ("fix the hand", "take away the extra antenna").
5. Make a **brand board** page in Google Slides or Canva: logo, colors with codes, 2 fonts, the mascot, and 3 do's and don'ts (like "Don't stretch the logo").

**Checkpoint 2 (+20 XP):** 3 scenes with the same mascot + brand board.

```solo
Let the student name the mascot and give it a personality. Ask: "What would your mascot do on a rainy day?" Use their answers as scene ideas.
```

## 4. Game / challenge — Glitch Hunt (15 min · 1-on-1: 10 min)
Show 6 Gemini pictures. Each has 1 to 3 glitches. Circle them. Kinds of glitches: **hands and fingers, words, shadows and light, things that can't happen, patterns that repeat, extra parts**.

```group
Houses circle glitches on printouts. Most found: +15 XP. Everyone: +5.
```

```solo
**Glitch race.** 1 minute per picture. The student circles glitches on the screen or a printout, and you check. Then they try to make a glitch on purpose in Gemini (hint: hands holding lots of things, or long words). +15 XP for playing, +5 for 6 or more glitches found.
```

## 5. Share-out — Who owns AI art? (10 min · 1-on-1: 5 min)
Choices: *The person who wrote the prompt / The AI company / The artists whose pictures the AI learned from / Nobody*. Pick one and give a reason.
Then share the facts: in many countries (like the US), a picture made only by AI can't be owned with copyright unless a person added a real creative part. Some artists have gone to court about AI learning from their work. The rules are still being decided. **Our class rule: make your own, say you used AI, and give credit.**

```group
Four corners. Students stand in a corner and give one reason.
```

```solo
**Switch sides.** The student picks a side and gives a reason. Then they must argue for a DIFFERENT side for 30 seconds. Then share the facts.
```

## 6. Exit ticket (5 min)
1. Name 2 ways to keep a character the same.
2. Name 3 common AI glitches.
3. Who do you think should own AI art? Give one reason.

```solo
Ask out loud. Write their opinion in your notes. It's fun to ask again at the end of the year.
```

## Differentiation
- **Extra help:** give a fill-in character sheet ("My mascot is a ___ with ___ and ___"). Make 2 scenes instead of 3.
- **Extra challenge:** make a 4-panel comic with the mascot. Does it stay the same in all 4?
- **1-on-1 tip:** the mascot comes back in later modules (video, website, games). Save the character sheet somewhere easy to find.
