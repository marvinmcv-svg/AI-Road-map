## Lesson objectives
Students will be able to…
- Apply **visual hierarchy** (1 big thing → headline → details → CTA) and **contrast**.
- Write a clear, honest **call to action (CTA)**.
- Design a social post and a poster that pass the **3-Second Test**.

## Vocabulary today
visual hierarchy · contrast · call to action (CTA) · typography · alignment · white/negative space · UGC (preview)

---

## 1. Warm-up — Which poster wins? (10 min)
Show the cluttered vs. clear poster side by side for 3 seconds each, then hide them. Ask: "What was each poster about? What did it want you to do?" Most remember only the clear one. Then quick Recall Raid: logo types + "scalable".

## 2. Mini-lesson — The 4 design moves (15 min)
Annotate the good poster live (**Visual 1**):
1. **Hierarchy:** one hero image or headline is biggest → supporting text → small details → CTA.
2. **Contrast:** light on dark or dark on light; check readability (WebAIM contrast checker ≥ 4.5:1 for small text).
3. **Alignment & space:** line things up; leave negative space — crowded = ignored.
4. **Typography:** max 2 fonts (one bold display font for headlines + one simple font for text).

**The CTA (Visual 2):** a short verb-first instruction: *Try it free · Join the club · Pre-order now · Scan to learn more*. It must be **honest** — no fake countdowns, no fake "only 2 left!", no fake reviews.

**Social formats:** feed post 1:1 or 4:5; story 9:16 (keep text away from the top/bottom 15% — app buttons cover it).

## 3. Hands-on build — Launch Post + Poster (35 min)
Using the Brand Kit from last lesson:
1. **Generate a hero image** for the brand (Image Prompt Formula, leave **negative space** for text, matching the brand colors).
2. **Social post (1:1 or 9:16):** hero image + headline (≤ 7 words) + CTA + logo + *#MadeWithAI*.
3. **Poster (4:5):** hero image + headline + 3 short benefit bullets + CTA + logo.

Checklist (on the board):
- [ ] One thing is clearly the biggest
- [ ] Max 2 fonts, brand colors only
- [ ] Text readable on the background (contrast)
- [ ] CTA starts with a verb
- [ ] No false claims; AI label included

**Checkpoint 1 (+20 XP):** social post done.
**Checkpoint 2 (+20 XP):** poster done.

## 4. Game / challenge — The 3-Second Test (15 min)
Pairs rotate: show your poster to a classmate for 3 seconds, hide it, ask: *"What's the brand? What's the product? What should you do?"* All 3 correct = +15 XP to the designer. If not, fix ONE thing and re-test (+5 XP for improving).

## 5. Share-out — Pro critique (10 min)
Teacher picks 3 volunteers' posters and the class critiques using the checklist (warm and cool feedback). Highlight a good CTA as Prompt-of-the-Week style "Design of the Week".

## 6. Exit ticket (5 min)
1. What is visual hierarchy?
2. Write a CTA for your brand.
3. Why is "Only 1 left — buy now!" a problem if it isn't true?

## Differentiation
- **Support:** use a pre-made Canva template and only swap image, headline and CTA; headline word bank.
- **Extension:** A/B test: make two versions of the post (different headline or CTA) and survey 5 classmates; calculate which "won" and explain why.
