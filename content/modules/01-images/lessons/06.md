## Lesson objectives
By the end, the student can…
- Make the most important thing **biggest** (one big thing → title → details → ask).
- Keep words **easy to read** on the background.
- Write a clear, honest **call to action**.
- Make a post and a poster that pass the **3-Second Test**.

## Words for today
- **Biggest first (visual hierarchy)** — the most important thing is the biggest. Your eyes go there first.
- **Contrast** — light on dark, or dark on light, so words pop.
- **Call to action (CTA)** — a short line that tells people what to do next ("Try it free!").
- **Font** — the style of the letters.
- **Lined up (alignment)** — things sit in neat lines, not all over the place.

## 1. Warm-up — Which poster wins? (10 min · 1-on-1: 5 min)
```teacher
An ad has about 3 seconds to grab someone. Today you will learn how to make the big thing big and the ask clear. Watch closely. I will only show these for a moment.
```

Show the messy poster and the clear poster for 3 seconds each. Then hide them. Ask: "What was each poster about? What did it want you to do?" Most people only remember the clear one. Then a quick recall: logo types, and what "works tiny" means.

```solo
After the 3 seconds, the student draws what they remember from each poster. Compare the drawings to the real posters.
```

## 2. Mini-lesson — The 4 design moves (15 min · 1-on-1: 10 min)
Mark up the good poster live (**Visual 1**):
1. **Biggest first:** one big picture or title is the biggest thing → then smaller words → then tiny details → then the ask.
2. **Contrast:** light on dark, or dark on light. Check that small words are easy to read.
3. **Line it up and leave space:** line things up. Leave empty space. Crowded = ignored.
4. **Fonts:** 2 fonts at most. One bold font for titles, one simple font for other words.

**The call to action (Visual 2):** a short line that starts with a doing word. *Try it free · Join the club · Order now · Scan to learn more*. It must be **honest**. No fake countdowns. No fake "only 2 left!". No fake reviews.

**Post shapes:** square 1:1 or 4:5 for posts. Tall 9:16 for phone stories. Keep words away from the very top and bottom of a story, because app buttons cover them.

## 3. Hands-on build — Launch Post + Poster (35 min · 1-on-1: 25 min)
Use the brand colors and logo from last lesson.
1. **Make a main picture in Gemini.** Use the formula. Leave **empty space** for words. Use the brand colors.
2. **Social post (1:1 or 9:16):** main picture + title (7 words or less) + call to action + logo + *#MadeWithAI*.
3. **Poster (4:5):** main picture + title + 3 short "why it's great" points + call to action + logo.

You can ask Gemini to add the title for you. Check the spelling. Or put the words on yourself in Google Slides or Canva.

Checklist:
- [ ] One thing is clearly the biggest
- [ ] 2 fonts at most, brand colors only
- [ ] Words are easy to read on the background
- [ ] The call to action starts with a doing word
- [ ] Nothing untrue, and the AI label is there

**Checkpoint 1 (+20 XP):** social post done.
**Checkpoint 2 (+20 XP):** poster done.

```solo
If time is short, do the post today and the poster as homework. Ask "What should people see FIRST?" before they start.
```

## 4. Game / challenge — The 3-Second Test (15 min · 1-on-1: 10 min)
Show the poster to someone for 3 seconds. Hide it. Ask: *"What's the brand? What does it sell? What should you do?"* All 3 right = +15 XP. If not, fix ONE thing and test again (+5 XP for making it better).

```group
Pairs rotate around the room.
```

```solo
You take the test first. Then grab one more tester if you can (a family member, another teacher, or a quick video call). Two testers = better info. Then the student gives YOUR poster the 3-Second Test.
```

## 5. Share-out — Pro check (10 min · 1-on-1: 5 min)
Check posters with the checklist. Give warm feedback (what works) and cool feedback (what to fix). Pick a "Design of the Week".

```group
Pick 3 volunteers' posters to check as a class.
```

```solo
The student checks their own poster with the checklist first, then you check it. Where do you agree?
```

## 6. Exit ticket (5 min)
1. What does "biggest first" mean?
2. Write a call to action for your brand.
3. Why is "Only 1 left, buy now!" a problem if it isn't true?

```solo
Ask out loud. Have them say their call to action like a radio ad.
```

## Differentiation
- **Extra help:** start from the blank starter page. Use the checklist as a step-by-step list. Make the post only.
- **Extra challenge:** make the same ad in 3 shapes (square, tall, wide). What has to move each time?
- **1-on-1 tip:** look at real ads together (cereal boxes, store signs). Find the biggest thing and the call to action on each one.
