## Lesson objectives
Students will be able to…
- Assemble a complete, consistent **Brand Kit** and self-assess it with the rubric.
- Pitch their brand in 60 seconds using design vocabulary.
- Give and receive rubric-based peer feedback.

## Vocabulary today
All Module 1 vocabulary — especially brand identity, visual hierarchy, CTA, scalability, disclosure.

---

## 1. Warm-up — Vocabulary lightning round (5 min)
Teacher shows a term; first house to give a definition + example scores. 10 terms, fast.

## 2. Mini-lesson — What "Pro" looks like (5 min)
Walk through the rubric's **4 · Pro** column for each criterion with one example from a previous student (or your own demo kit). Remind: every asset needs a disclosure label; the study aid must have the Fact-Check Stamp.

## 3. Hands-on build — Final assembly + self-check (35 min)
Build the 6-slide **Brand Kit deck** in Canva:
1. **Brand Brief** (name, audience, personality words, mission)
2. **Logo** (combination mark + icon-only + small-size test)
3. **Brand board** (colors with hex, fonts, mascot, do's & don'ts)
4. **Social post + poster**
5. **Study aid** (with Fact-Check Stamp)
6. **Behind the prompts** (best prompt log entry: v1 → final, what changed and why)

Self-assessment: students score themselves on the rubric and write one sentence of evidence per row. Last 5 minutes: fix the lowest row.
**Checkpoint (+20 XP):** deck complete + self-assessment filled in.

## 4. Game / challenge — The Pitch Arena (30 min)
60-second pitches (split into 2 rooms/groups if the class is large):
- "Our brand is ___ for ___. Our personality is ___, ___, ___. We chose ___ because ___."
- Audience fills peer score cards (one strength, one question).
- Teacher (and guest judge) scores with the rubric.

**Boss XP:** rubric total (4 criteria × 1–4) × 6 ≈ up to 96 → round to 100 for a perfect score. **🎨 Pixel Forger** badge for 3+ on every row. House awards: "Best Logo", "Best CTA", "Most Consistent Mascot" (+15 house XP each).

## 5. Share-out — Brand Wall (10 min)
Print or display all logos together as the "Guild Client Wall". Announce: *"Your client is happy… and now they need a launch VIDEO."* Preview Module 2.

## 6. Exit ticket (5 min)
1. Which asset are you most proud of and why?
2. What's one design skill you'll use again in the video and website modules?
3. What would you change if you had one more lesson?

## Differentiation
- **Support:** reduced kit (logo + post + study aid) is still eligible for "Proficient"; pitch script with blanks; option to pitch to the teacher only.
- **Extension:** Level-up challenge — mascot in 3 consistent scenes + an animated sticker/GIF (+50 XP); act as a guest judge with the rubric for another house.
