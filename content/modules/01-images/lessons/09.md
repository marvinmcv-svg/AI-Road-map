## Lesson objectives
By the end, the student can…
- Put together a whole **Brand Kit** that looks like it belongs together, and score it with the rubric.
- Pitch the brand in 60 seconds using design words.
- Give and get feedback using the rubric.

## Words for today
All the Module 1 words. Most of all: brand, biggest first, call to action, works tiny, #MadeWithAI.

---

## 1. Warm-up — Word lightning round (5 min)
Show a word. Give its meaning + an example. 10 words, fast.

```group
First house to answer scores.
```

```solo
Use the flashcards. Time it: how many can the student get in 2 minutes? Then try to beat that score.
```

## 2. Mini-lesson — What "Pro" looks like (5 min)
Go through the rubric's **4 · Pro** column, one row at a time. Show one example (your own demo kit is great). Remind: every piece needs an AI label. The study helper needs the Fact-Check Stamp.

## 3. Hands-on build — Put it all together + check (35 min · 1-on-1: 25 min)
Build the 6-slide **Brand Kit deck**:
1. **Brand Brief** (name, buyers, personality words, mission)
2. **Logo** (icon + name, icon only, and the tiny-size test)
3. **Brand board** (colors with codes, fonts, mascot, do's and don'ts)
4. **Social post + poster**
5. **Study helper** (with the Fact-Check Stamp)
6. **Behind the prompts** (the best Prompt Log entry: first try → last try, what changed and why)

Self-check: score yourself on the rubric. Write one sentence of proof for each row. Last 5 minutes: fix the lowest row.
**Checkpoint (+20 XP):** deck done + self-check filled in.

```solo
Let the student self-score first, with no help. Then compare with your score. Where you disagree, talk it out. That's the best learning of the module.
```

## 4. Game / challenge — The Pitch Arena (30 min · 1-on-1: 15 min)
A 60-second pitch:
- "Our brand is ___ for ___. Our personality is ___, ___ and ___. We picked ___ because ___."
- Listeners fill in a score card (one strength, one question).
- The teacher scores with the rubric.

**Boss XP:** rubric total (4 rows × 1 to 4 points) × 6 = up to 96. Round it up to 100 for a perfect score. **🎨 Pixel Forger** badge for 3 or more on every row.

```group
Split into 2 groups if the class is big. House awards: "Best Logo", "Best Call to Action", "Most Same-Every-Time Mascot" (+15 house XP each).
```

```solo
**Shark tank.** You and the guests are the "investors". The student pitches. Each guest asks one question. Then do a second pitch after their fixes. Most people are way better the second time. Awards for 1-on-1: pick the student's best piece for "Best in Show" (+15 XP).
```

## 5. Share-out — Brand Wall (10 min · 1-on-1: 5 min)
Print or show all the brand pieces together as the "Guild Client Wall". Then tease the next quest: *"Your client loves it… and now they need a launch VIDEO."*

```solo
Make a one-page "Brand Wall" for the student to take home or share. Ask them to show their family.
```

## 6. Exit ticket (5 min)
1. Which piece are you most proud of? Why?
2. What design skill will you use again in the video and website modules?
3. What would you change if you had one more lesson?

```solo
Ask out loud. Save the answers. They're great for a progress note to the family.
```

## Differentiation
- **Extra help:** use the starter deck with slide titles and hints. A 4-slide kit is OK (brief, logo, post, behind the prompts).
- **Extra challenge:** add a 7th slide: a "brand rules" page for another designer. Or record the pitch as a video.
- **1-on-1 tip:** a real audience matters a lot. Even one guest on a video call makes the pitch feel real.
