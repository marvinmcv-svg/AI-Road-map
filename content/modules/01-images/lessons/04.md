## Lesson objectives
By the end, the student can…
- Ask Gemini to **remove the background**, **swap one thing**, **make it wider** and **make it sharper**.
- Say the difference between an **honest edit** and a **tricky edit**.
- Explain why changing photos of real people without a "yes" hurts (**deepfakes**), and add an AI label.

## Words for today
- **Remove background** — cut out the thing and get rid of what's behind it.
- **Swap (fill)** — pick one part and tell AI what should go there instead.
- **Expand** — make the picture wider or taller. AI fills in the new edges.
- **Upscale** — make a small picture bigger and sharper.
- **Deepfake** — a fake picture or video of a real person.
- **Consent** — a clear "yes" from the person.

## 1. Warm-up — Real or Edited? (10 min · 1-on-1: 5 min)
```teacher
AI can change a photo in seconds. That is a super power, and it comes with a rule: be honest about it. Let's see if you can spot the edits.
```

Show 6 pictures of objects and places. Some are edited. Real or edited? Point to the clue: shadows, edges, patterns that repeat. Then reveal. Ask: "If you can't tell, how would anyone online know?"

```solo
The student holds a thumbs up (real) or down (edited) for each picture, then points to the clue. Keep score. Can they beat 4 out of 6?
```

## 2. Mini-lesson — The four AI edits (15 min · 1-on-1: 10 min)
Show it live on ONE product picture (like a shoe) in **Gemini**. Show **Visual 1**. Ask for each change in the same chat:
1. **Remove background:** "Put the shoe on a plain white background."
2. **Swap one thing:** "Take away the stain on the toe." or "Add a small plant next to it."
3. **Expand:** "Make this picture wide, 16:9, with empty space on the left."
4. **Upscale:** "Make this sharper and more clear."

**Honest vs. tricky** (**Visual 2**):
- ✅ Clean the background, fix the light, put the product in a new scene **and label it**.
- ❌ Make the product look bigger or better than it really is, fake "results", fake buyers, change a real person's face or body.

**Consent & deepfakes:** with a few clicks, someone's face can be put into a fake scene. Even "jokes" can hurt people. They can spread forever. In many places they break the law. **Our rule:** we edit objects, places and made-up characters. Never real people. Gemini adds a hidden mark (called SynthID) to its pictures. That's one way apps try to label AI pictures.

## 3. Hands-on build — Product Photo Glow-Up (35 min · 1-on-1: 25 min)
Pick one object to stand in for your company's product.

| Step | What to ask Gemini | Save it as |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Make a picture of the product: "A [product], plain white background, close-up, 1:1" | `product-plain.png` |
| 2 | Move it into a new scene that fits the brand: "Same [product], now on a [place], [style], [light]" | `product-scene.png` |
| 3 | Swap one thing: add or take away one detail | `product-swap.png` |
| 4 | Expand to a wide 16:9 banner with empty space for words | `product-banner.png` |
| 5 | Add an "AI-edited" label | — |

**Checkpoint 1 (+20 XP):** steps 1 and 2 done.
**Checkpoint 2 (+20 XP):** steps 3 to 5 done + one sentence: "This edit is honest because…"

**Bonus with a real photo:** take a photo of the real object at the photo corner. Upload it to Gemini and do steps 2 to 4 on it. Compare the real-photo version with the made-up one.

```group
Work in pairs. One types, one checks the log. Swap at step 3.
```

```solo
The student does steps 1 to 4 in Gemini, then the real-photo bonus. Ask before each edit: "Is this still honest?"
```

> 🛡️ **Safety moment:** Only objects. If a face shows up in the background of a real photo, retake the photo.

## 4. Game / challenge — Honest or Tricky? Ad Court (15 min · 1-on-1: 10 min)
Be the judge. For each ad, pick **Honest**, **Tricky** or **Needs a label**. Give a reason with today's words.
1. A burger photo made 3 times bigger than the real one. → Tricky
2. A water bottle placed on a mountain scene, labeled "AI-edited background". → Honest
3. A before-and-after skin cream photo made with AI. → Tricky (fake results)
4. A shoe's colors made a bit brighter, to match how it really looks in good light. → Honest
5. An AI-made "happy buyer" photo with a made-up quote. → Tricky (fake buyer)
6. A banner made wider with AI so it fits a website. → Honest (add a label)

```group
Houses judge. Most right with the best reasons: +15 XP. Everyone who finishes: +5.
```

```solo
**Courtroom.** You play the sneaky ad maker and "argue" for each ad. The student is the judge and must say why it's honest or tricky. For cards 1, 3 and 5, let the student redo the ad so it's honest. +15 XP for playing, +5 for good reasons.
```

## 5. Share-out — Before & After wall (10 min · 1-on-1: 5 min)
Show the before and after pictures. Guess which edit made each change.

```solo
Line up all 4 pictures. You guess which edit the student used for each one. They tell you if you're right.
```

## 6. Exit ticket (5 min)
1. Match each word to its meaning: remove background / swap / expand / upscale.
2. Give one honest edit and one tricky edit.
3. Why do we never edit photos of real people in this class?

```solo
Ask out loud. For #1, point at their 4 pictures and let them name each edit.
```

## Differentiation
- **Extra help:** give the exact Gemini sentence for each step, with blanks to fill in. Do steps 1 to 3 only.
- **Extra challenge:** make the product look good in 3 places (a beach, a desk, space) with the same light and style. Is it still the same product every time?
- **1-on-1 tip:** if the student wants to edit a photo of themselves or a friend, use it as a teaching moment. Talk about consent, then pick an object instead.
