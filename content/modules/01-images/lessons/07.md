## Lesson objectives
By the end, the student can…
- Explain why **words + pictures** help you remember.
- Make a set of flashcards and a labeled diagram or picture note for a real school topic.
- Check every label and fact with the **Fact-Check Stamp** (2 trusted sources).

## Words for today
- **Words + pictures (dual coding)** — learning with both helps your brain remember.
- **Self-quiz (retrieval practice)** — testing yourself. The #1 way to study.
- **Diagram** — a picture with labels and arrows that shows parts or steps.
- **Picture notes (sketchnote)** — one page of notes with drawings, arrows and boxes.
- **Hallucination** (again) — AI saying something false like it's true.

## 1. Warm-up — Spot the AI Mistake (10 min · 1-on-1: 5 min)
Show a Gemini diagram of a plant cell (or the water cycle) with one wrong label or a jumbled word. Find the mistake AND show a source that proves it. Ask: "What if you had studied from this for a test?"

```group
First house to find it with proof: +15 XP each.
```

```solo
Set a 3-minute timer. If the student finds it with proof: +15 XP. Then switch: they ask Gemini for a diagram of something they know really well. Can they catch a mistake in it?
```

## 2. Mini-lesson — Why pictures help you learn (12 min · 1-on-1: 8 min)
**Visual 1 (Words + pictures):** your brain keeps words and pictures in two paths that link up. A study helper with both = two roads to the memory.

Three kinds of study helpers:
1. **Flashcard:** the word and a simple picture on the front. The meaning and an example on the back. Then test yourself. That's a **self-quiz**, the best way to study.
2. **Labeled diagram:** a clear picture with arrows and labels (parts or steps).
3. **Picture notes:** one page with small drawings, arrows and boxes.

**AI warning:** picture AIs can make **wrong labels, made-up words and extra parts**. The safe way:
1. Get the **facts** from your textbook. (Or ask Gemini, then check each one.)
2. Make the **picture with no words**: add "no text, no labels" to the prompt.
3. Add the labels yourself in Google Slides or Canva. Or ask Gemini to add them, then check every one.

## 3. Hands-on build — Study Helper Studio (38 min · 1-on-1: 27 min)
Pick a real topic from school (or a topic for your company's buyers, like "how to care for a plant").
1. **Facts first (8 min · 1-on-1: 6 min):** write 6 key words and their meanings from the textbook or a trusted site. If Gemini helps, check each one.
2. **Flashcards (15 min · 1-on-1: 11 min):** make 6 flashcards. Picture prompt example: *"Simple flat icon of a chloroplast, green, clean white background, no text, for a school lesson, 1:1."*
3. **Diagram or picture notes (15 min · 1-on-1: 10 min):** one labeled diagram, or a one-page picture note with arrows.

**Fact-Check Stamp** (put it in the corner of every helper):
- [ ] Every label checked in 2 trusted sources
- [ ] No jumbled or made-up words
- [ ] The picture matches the real thing (number of parts, colors, order)
- [ ] #MadeWithAI + where the facts came from

**Checkpoint 1 (+20 XP):** 6 flashcards with the stamp.
**Checkpoint 2 (+20 XP):** diagram or picture notes with the stamp.

```solo
This is a great time to help with the student's real school work. Use a topic from their next test. They'll get a study helper they can really use.
```

## 4. Game / challenge — Flashcard Duel (15 min · 1-on-1: 10 min)
Swap flashcards and quiz each other. Look at the front, say the answer, flip. Each right answer = 1 point. Any card that was confusing or wrong = feedback for the maker.

```group
Pairs from different houses swap sets. Top house +15 XP. Everyone +5.
```

```solo
**Student vs. Bolt.** The student quizzes YOU with their cards first. (Get some wrong on purpose so they have to explain.) Then you quiz them. Then shuffle and go again, faster. +15 XP for playing, +5 if they get 5 or more right.
```

## 5. Share-out — Which helper helped most? (10 min · 1-on-1: 5 min)
Flashcard, diagram or picture notes: which one helped you remember? Why? Share the best ones with the school teacher (only with permission).

```solo
Ask: "Which one will you really use to study?" Send it home with them (printed or shared).
```

## 6. Exit ticket (5 min)
1. Why do words + pictures help you remember?
2. Why make pictures with "no text" and add labels yourself?
3. Write 2 steps of the Fact-Check Stamp.

```solo
Ask out loud. Then the student quizzes themselves with 3 of their own flashcards.
```

## Differentiation
- **Extra help:** 4 flashcards instead of 6. Give the facts list ready-made so they can focus on pictures.
- **Extra challenge:** make a 5-question quiz from your cards, with one trick question. Or turn the diagram into a "fill in the blank" sheet.
- **1-on-1 tip:** come back to these flashcards at the start of the next 2 lessons for a 2-minute self-quiz.
