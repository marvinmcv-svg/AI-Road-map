## Lesson objectives
Students will be able to…
- Explain **dual coding** and why pictures + words help memory.
- Create a flashcard set and a labelled diagram/visual note for a real school topic.
- Fact-check every label and fact using the **Fact-Check Stamp** (2 trusted sources).

## Vocabulary today
dual coding · retrieval practice · diagram · infographic · sketchnote · hallucination (review)

---

## 1. Warm-up — Spot the AI Mistake (10 min)
Show an AI-generated diagram of a plant cell (or the water cycle) with one wrong label or garbled word. First house to find the error AND show a source that proves it: +15 XP each member. Ask: "What if you'd studied from this for a test?"

## 2. Mini-lesson — Why pictures help you learn (12 min)
**Visual 1 (Dual coding):** your brain stores words and images in two linked "channels". A study aid with both = two roads to the memory.
Three study-aid formats:
1. **Flashcard:** term + simple picture on front; definition + example on back. Test yourself (that's **retrieval practice**, the #1 study method).
2. **Labelled diagram:** a clear picture with arrows and labels (processes, parts).
3. **Visual note / sketchnote:** a one-page summary with icons, arrows and boxes.

**AI warning:** image AIs often make **wrong labels, invented words, extra parts**. Best workflow:
1. Get the **facts** from your textbook (or AI text, then verify).
2. Generate the **picture without text** ("no text, no labels").
3. Add the labels yourself in Canva.

## 3. Hands-on build — Study Aid Studio (38 min)
Each student picks a real topic from another class (or the startup's customer topic, e.g. "how to care for a plant").
1. **Facts first (8 min):** list 6 key terms + definitions from the textbook/trusted site. If AI helps summarise, verify each one.
2. **Flashcards (15 min):** create 6 flashcards in Canva. Image prompt example: *"Simple flat icon of a chloroplast, green, clean white background, no text, educational style, 1:1."*
3. **Diagram or visual note (15 min):** one labelled diagram or a one-page sketchnote with arrows. Labels added in Canva.

**Fact-Check Stamp** (bottom corner of every aid):
- [ ] Every label checked in 2 trusted sources
- [ ] No garbled or invented text
- [ ] Image matches the real thing (number of parts, colors, order)
- [ ] #MadeWithAI + source names

**Checkpoint 1 (+20 XP):** 6 flashcards with stamp.
**Checkpoint 2 (+20 XP):** diagram/visual note with stamp.

## 4. Game / challenge — Flashcard Duel (15 min)
Pairs from different houses swap flashcard sets and quiz each other (front → say the answer → flip). Each correct answer = 1 point for the answerer's house; each card that was confusing or wrong = feedback for the creator. Top house +15 XP; all +5.

## 5. Share-out — Which aid helped most? (10 min)
Quick poll: flashcard vs. diagram vs. sketchnote — which helped you remember the swapped topic? Discuss why. Collect the best aids to send to the subject teacher (with permission).

## 6. Exit ticket (5 min)
1. What is dual coding?
2. Why should you generate pictures with "no text" and add labels yourself?
3. Write 2 steps of the Fact-Check Stamp.

## Differentiation
- **Support:** use the teacher-provided term list; flashcard template with picture box and two lines; diagram outline pre-drawn.
- **Extension:** make a spaced-repetition schedule for the flashcards (day 1, 3, 7, 14) and explain why spacing works; create a 5-question quiz from the visual note.
