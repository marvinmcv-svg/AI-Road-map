## Lesson objectives
Students will be able to…
- Name and use 5+ **shot types/camera angles** in prompts.
- Apply the **rule of thirds** and choose an **aspect ratio** for a purpose.
- Identify 6 common art **styles** and describe a style without copying an artist.

## Vocabulary today
composition · rule of thirds · shot type · camera angle · aspect ratio · style · negative space

---

## 1. Warm-up — Formula recall + "Where's the subject?" (10 min)
Recall Raid: list the 6 Image Prompt Formula parts. Then show 3 photos/paintings; students point to where the main subject sits. Introduce the question: "Why didn't the artist put it in the middle?"

## 2. Mini-lesson — The photographer's toolkit (15 min)
Show **Visual 1 (Shot chart)** with one subject (a skateboarding robot) in each shot:
- **Wide shot** — shows the place (good for setting the scene).
- **Medium shot** — waist up (good for actions/characters).
- **Close-up / extreme close-up** — emotion and detail (good for products).
- **Bird's-eye view** — from above (maps, flat-lays, food).
- **Low angle** — looking up (makes things look powerful/heroic).

Show **Visual 2 (Rule of thirds)**: 3×3 grid. Subject on a line or crossing; **negative space** (empty area) leaves room for text — *crucial for posters and ads later.*

**Aspect ratio by purpose:**
| Ratio | Use |
|---|---|
| 1:1 | Feed post, profile picture, logo |
| 9:16 | Phone story, reel, TikTok-style |
| 16:9 | Slides, YouTube thumbnail, website banner |
| 4:5 / 3:4 | Posters, portrait posts |

**Style vocabulary:** pixel art, flat vector, watercolor, 3D clay, comic/ink, photorealistic, low-poly, paper cut-out. Remind: describe a style; never "in the style of [living artist]".

## 3. Hands-on build — The Shot List (35 min)
Students pick ONE subject (e.g. "a tiny dragon baking cupcakes") and create a **4-image shot list**, like a film director:

| Shot | Purpose | Prompt (full formula) |
|---|---|---|
| Wide shot, 16:9 | Show the setting | |
| Medium shot, 1:1 | Show the action | |
| Close-up, 4:5, subject on right third with negative space on the left | Poster with room for a title | |
| Bird's-eye view, 1:1 | Flat-lay of the cupcakes | |

**Checkpoint 1 (+20 XP):** shot list prompts written BEFORE generating (planning!).
**Checkpoint 2 (+20 XP):** 4 images generated, placed in a Canva grid, each labelled with shot type + ratio.

Teacher tip: circulate and ask "Why that shot? What does the viewer feel?"

## 4. Game / challenge — Style Roulette (15 min)
Spin the wheel for: **style + shot type + aspect ratio** (e.g. "watercolor + low angle + 9:16"). Houses have 3 minutes to write a prompt for a shared subject that uses the spin. Teacher generates 1 per house; class votes "Does it match the spin?" Correct match +15 XP; all finishers +5.

## 5. Share-out — Director's commentary (10 min)
Pairs swap shot lists. Each student explains ONE choice: "I used a low angle because I wanted the dragon to look heroic."

## 6. Exit ticket (5 min)
1. Show 3 small images → label the shot type for each.
2. Which aspect ratio would you use for a phone story? For a slide?
3. What is negative space useful for?
4. **Startup pick:** choose your Guild client — invent one or pick from the list (eco water bottles, dog-walking app, bakery for robots, plant-care kit, skate-repair shop, study-snack box). Write: name idea + product + who the customers are. (You'll build the full Brand Brief in lesson 5.)

## Visuals to show
**Visual 1 — Shot type chart.**
```prompt
An educational chart, 16:9, titled "SHOT TYPES & CAMERA ANGLES". Five panels showing the same cute skateboarding robot character: "WIDE SHOT" robot small in a city skate park; "MEDIUM SHOT" robot from waist up; "CLOSE-UP" robot's face with a big grin; "BIRD'S-EYE VIEW" from directly above; "LOW ANGLE" looking up at the robot heroically against the sky. Consistent friendly 3D cartoon style, clear bold labels under each panel, white background.
```

**Visual 2 — Rule of thirds overlay.**
```prompt
A photo-style illustration of a lighthouse on a cliff at sunset, 16:9, with a clear 3x3 white grid overlaid. The lighthouse sits exactly on the right vertical line, the horizon on the lower horizontal line. Glowing dots mark the four intersection points. Large empty sky on the left labelled "NEGATIVE SPACE (room for text!)". Title at top: "RULE OF THIRDS". Clean, educational, bold labels.
```

**Visual 3 — Aspect ratio cheat sheet.**
```prompt
A simple infographic, 16:9, titled "PICK YOUR SHAPE". Four rectangles drawn to scale side by side: a square "1:1 feed post", a tall phone "9:16 story/reel", a wide screen "16:9 slide & thumbnail", a portrait "4:5 poster". Each contains a tiny icon of its use. Flat pastel colors, bold labels, white background.
```

## Differentiation
- **Support:** shot cards with pictures to copy into prompts; reduce shot list to 3 images.
- **Extension:** add lens/photography words (macro, shallow depth of field, fisheye) and record which ones the AI understands; analyse composition in a famous public-domain painting.
