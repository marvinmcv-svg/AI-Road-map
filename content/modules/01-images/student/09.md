## 🔥 Warm-up: Vocabulary Lightning Round
Your teacher shows a word. First house with a definition + example scores!

## 💡 What "Pro" looks like
Check the rubric with your teacher. Every asset needs a **#MadeWithAI** label, and your study aid needs its **Fact-Check Stamp**.

## 🛠️ Your build: 6-slide Brand Kit
- [ ] Slide 1: Brand Brief (name, customers, 3 personality words, mission)
- [ ] Slide 2: Logo + icon-only version + tiny-size test
- [ ] Slide 3: Brand board (colours, fonts, mascot, do's & don'ts)
- [ ] Slide 4: Social post + poster
- [ ] Slide 5: Study aid with Fact-Check Stamp
- [ ] Slide 6: Behind the prompts (your best v1 → final prompt and why it improved)
- [ ] Score yourself on the rubric and fix your lowest row

## ⚔️ Challenge: The Pitch Arena
60 seconds on stage:
> "Our brand is ___ for ___. Our personality is ___, ___, ___. We chose ___ because ___. Our call to action is ___."

Give every presenter one ⭐ strength and one ❓ question on your peer card.

## 🎟️ Exit ticket
1. Which asset are you most proud of, and why?
2. Which design skill will you use again for video and websites?
3. What would you change with one more lesson?

## 🛡️ Stay safe
Everything in your kit must be original — no real brands, no real people.
