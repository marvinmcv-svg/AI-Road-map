## 🔥 Warm-up: Word Lightning Round
Your teacher shows a word. Say what it means and give an example. Fast!

## 💡 What "Pro" looks like
Look at the rubric with your teacher. Every piece needs a **#MadeWithAI** label. Your study helper needs its **Fact-Check Stamp**.

## 🛠️ Your build: 6-slide Brand Kit
- [ ] Slide 1: Brand Brief (name, buyers, 3 personality words, mission)
- [ ] Slide 2: Logo + icon-only version + tiny-size test
- [ ] Slide 3: Brand board (colors, fonts, mascot, do's and don'ts)
- [ ] Slide 4: Social post + poster
- [ ] Slide 5: Study helper with the Fact-Check Stamp
- [ ] Slide 6: Behind the prompts (your best first try → last try, and why it got better)
- [ ] Score yourself with the rubric. Fix your lowest row.

## ⚔️ Challenge: The Pitch Arena
60 seconds on stage:
> "Our brand is ___ for ___. Our personality is ___, ___ and ___. We picked ___ because ___. Our call to action is ___."

```group
Give every presenter one ⭐ strength and one ❓ question on your score card.
```

```solo
Pitch to your teacher and a guest. They each ask you one question. Then fix one thing and pitch again. You'll be even better the second time!
```

## 🎟️ Exit ticket
1. Which piece are you most proud of? Why?
2. Which design skill will you use again for videos and websites?
3. What would you change if you had one more lesson?

## 🛡️ Stay safe
Everything in your kit must be your own. No real brands, no real people.
