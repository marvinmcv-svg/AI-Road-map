## 🔥 Warm-up
Name the 4 AI edits. Then draw a famous logo from memory in 30 seconds. Why was it easy to remember?

## 💡 Big idea: what makes a logo work
| Logo type | What it is |
|---|---|
| 🔤 Word logo | The name in a cool font |
| 🅰️ Letter logo | Just the first letters |
| ⭐ Icon | Just a symbol |
| ⭐🔤 Icon + name | Symbol and name together (we'll make this!) |
| 🛡️ Badge | The name inside a shape |

**The 4 logo rules:** simple · works tiny AND huge · easy to remember · fits your brand.

**What colors make people feel:** 💙 calm and trust · 💚 nature · 💛 fun · ❤️ exciting · 💜 creative · 🖤 fancy

## 🛠️ Your build: Brand Brief → Logo
- [ ] Brand Brief: name, what it sells, who buys it
- [ ] Brand Brief: 3 personality words + a mission ("We help ___ to ___ by ___.")
- [ ] Make 6 icon ideas in Gemini
- [ ] Pick the best 2 and ask for one fix each ("make it simpler")
- [ ] Ask Gemini to add your brand name. Check every letter!
- [ ] Make 3 versions: side by side, stacked, icon only
- [ ] Write down your brand color codes (like #1CB0F6)

```try
Simple flat logo icon of a water drop shaped like a leaf, 2 colors (teal and lime), thick round lines, in the middle of a white background, no words, simple enough to be an app icon, square 1:1.
```

## ⚔️ Challenge: Squint & Shrink
Make your logo tiny. Look at it from 3 big steps away and squint. Can you still tell what it is? Can you read the name?

```group
A partner from another house tests your logo.
```

```solo
Your teacher tests your logo. Then you test a messy logo your teacher made. Can you tell Gemini how to fix it?
```

## 🎟️ Exit ticket
1. Name 3 logo types.
2. What does "works tiny" mean?
3. Why did you pick your main brand color?

## 🛡️ Stay safe
Your logo must not look like a real brand. No swooshes, no bitten apples, no golden arches. Make your own!
