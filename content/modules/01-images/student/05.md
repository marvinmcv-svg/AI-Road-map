## 🔥 Warm-up
Draw a famous logo from memory in 30 seconds. Why could you remember it? (Simple shapes! Few colours!)

## 💡 Big idea: 5 kinds of logos
| Type | What it is |
|---|---|
| 🔤 Wordmark | the name in a cool font |
| 🅰️ Lettermark | just the initials |
| 🦊 Icon | a picture symbol only |
| 🔗 **Combination mark** | icon + name — **we're making this one!** |
| 🛡️ Emblem | name inside a badge |

**Logo rules:** simple · works tiny AND huge · memorable · fits your brand's personality.
**Colour vibes:** 💙 trust · 💚 nature · 💛 fun · 🧡 energy · ❤️ excitement · 💜 creative · 🖤 premium

## 🛠️ Your build
**Brand Brief**
- [ ] Brand name
- [ ] Product or service
- [ ] Customers: who are they, what do they care about?
- [ ] 3 personality words (e.g. playful, eco, bold)
- [ ] Mission: "We help ___ to ___ by ___."

**Logo**
- [ ] Generate 6 icon ideas (no text!)
- [ ] Pick 2 and improve each once ("simpler", "thicker lines", "only 2 colours")
- [ ] In Canva, add your brand name in a real font
- [ ] Make 3 versions: side-by-side, stacked, icon-only
- [ ] Save your colours (with hex codes) in the Canva Brand Kit

```try
Minimal flat vector logo icon of a water drop shaped like a leaf, 2 colors (teal and lime), thick rounded lines, centered on white, no text, simple enough to be an app icon, 1:1.
```

## ⚔️ Challenge: Squint & Shrink
Shrink your logo to tiny (16px). Squint from 3 metres. Can a partner still tell what it is?

## 🎟️ Exit ticket
1. Name 3 logo types.
2. What does "scalable" mean?
3. Why did you choose your main colour?

## 🛡️ Stay safe
Your logo must not look like a real brand's. Copying logos is unfair — and can get companies sued!
