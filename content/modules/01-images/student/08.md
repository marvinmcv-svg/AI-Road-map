## 💡 Big idea: the consistency problem
Every AI image starts from new random noise, so your character can change every time! 😱 Fix it with:
- 📝 a **fixed description** you copy-paste every time
- 🎨 a **fixed style line**
- 🖼️ a **reference image** (your best first image)
- 🔄 a **character sheet** (front, side, back)

```try
Bolt, a small round teal robot with one big yellow eye, orange antenna, short stubby legs, white belly panel with a leaf logo. Flat vector, thick outlines, soft shadows, teal/lime/white colors. Now show the same character skateboarding.
```
