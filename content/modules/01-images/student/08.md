## 💡 Big idea: the same character every time
Every AI picture starts from new static. So your character can change a little every time! Here's how to keep it the same:
- 📝 **Character sheet** — the exact words that describe your character. Copy them every time.
- 🎨 **Style line** — the same style words every time.
- 💬 **Same chat** — stay in the same Gemini chat and say "Same character, now…"
- 🔄 **Turnaround** — ask for front, side and back views on one page.

**AI glitches to look for:** ✋ extra fingers · 🔤 melted letters · 🌑 wrong shadows · 🌀 things that can't be real · ➕ extra parts
