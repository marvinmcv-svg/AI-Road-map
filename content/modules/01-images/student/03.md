## 🔥 Warm-up
Shot quiz! Then: what meme *formats* do you know? (before/after, expectation vs. reality, "POV:"…)

## 💡 Big idea: meme anatomy
1. 🖼️ **Image** — an instantly readable picture (big expressions!)
2. 🎬 **Setup** — a situation everyone knows
3. 💥 **Punchline** — the twist
4. 🙋 **Relatable** — "that's SO me!"
5. 🔤 **Format** — square, big bold text, few words

**Kindness rule:** memes punch at *situations*, never at *people*. ✅ animals, objects, Mondays · ❌ classmates, teachers, celebrities, copied characters.

## 🛠️ Your build: Brand Meme Lab
- [ ] Write 3 setups your customers relate to
- [ ] Pick a meme format for each
- [ ] Generate the images (close-up, big expression, plain background, 1:1)
- [ ] Add the text in **Canva** (AI text often comes out garbled!)
- [ ] Add a small **#MadeWithAI**
- [ ] Ask yourself: could anyone feel targeted? If yes, change it

```try
Close-up of a fluffy orange cat wearing a tiny chef hat, looking shocked at a burnt smoking cake, cartoon style, plain pastel background, 1:1, no text.
```

## ⚔️ Challenge: Caption Battle
One funny image, 3 minutes, best caption wins (it has to be kind!).

## 🎟️ Exit ticket
1. Name 3 parts of a meme.
2. Why add text in Canva instead of inside the AI image?
3. Is a meme using a photo of the principal OK? Why?

## 🛡️ Stay safe
If a meme could embarrass a real person, it's not a meme — it's bullying.
