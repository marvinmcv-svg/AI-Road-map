## 🔥 Warm-up
Shot quiz! Then: what kinds of memes do you know? (before and after, what I wanted vs. what I got, "POV:"…)

## 💡 Big idea: the parts of a meme
1. 🖼️ **Picture** — easy to "get" in one second (big silly faces!)
2. 🎬 **Setup** — a moment everyone knows
3. 💥 **Twist** — the funny surprise
4. 🙋 **"That's so me!"** — people see themselves in it
5. 🔤 **Shape** — square, big bold words, not too many words

**Kindness rule:** memes joke about *moments*, never about *people*.
✅ animals, things, Mondays · ❌ classmates, teachers, famous people, other people's characters

## 🛠️ Your build: Brand Meme Lab
- [ ] Write 3 setups your buyers know well
- [ ] Pick a meme type for each one
- [ ] Make the pictures in Gemini (close-up, big silly face, plain background, 1:1)
- [ ] Add the words (ask Gemini, or add them yourself in Slides or Canva)
- [ ] Check every letter. AI sometimes mixes up letters!
- [ ] Add a small **#MadeWithAI**
- [ ] Ask yourself: could anyone feel picked on? If yes, change it.


## ⚔️ Challenge: Caption Battle
One funny picture. 3 minutes. The best caption wins. It has to be kind!

```group
Your house writes one caption. The class votes.
```

```solo
**You vs. your teacher.** You both write a caption for 3 pictures. A family member or another teacher picks the funniest!
```

## 🎟️ Exit ticket
1. Name 3 parts of a meme.
2. Why should you check every letter when AI writes words on a picture?
3. Is a meme with a photo of the principal OK? Why or why not?

## 🛡️ Stay safe
If a meme could make a real person feel bad, it's not a meme. It's bullying.
