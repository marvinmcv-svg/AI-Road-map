## 💡 Big idea
Image AI starts with **random static (noise)** and cleans it up step by step until it matches your prompt — like a sculptor finding a statue in a block of marble. That's why the same prompt can give different pictures!

**The Image Prompt Formula**
| Part | Ask yourself | Try words like |
|---|---|---|
| ⭐ Subject | Who/what? Doing what? | a red fox reading a map |
| 📍 Setting | Where/when? | snowy forest at dawn |
| 🎨 Style | What art style? | pixel art, watercolor, 3D clay |
| 📷 Composition | How close? From where? | close-up, wide shot, bird's-eye |
| ☀️ Lighting | What mood? | golden hour, neon, pastel |
| 📐 Format | What shape? | square 1:1, story 9:16, slide 16:9 |

```try
A red fox reading a paper map in a snowy forest at dawn, watercolor illustration, wide shot with the fox on the left third, soft golden light, 16:9.
```
