## 💡 Big idea
Picture AI starts with **noise**. That's fuzzy static, like a TV with no signal. Then it cleans the static, step by step, until it matches your words. It's like fog clearing to show a picture. Every picture starts from new static. That's why the same prompt gives a new picture each time!

**The Image Prompt Formula**
| Part | Ask yourself | Try words like |
|---|---|---|
| ⭐ Subject | Who or what? Doing what? | a red fox reading a map |
| 📍 Setting | Where? When? | snowy forest at sunrise |
| 🎨 Style | What art look? | pixel art, watercolor, 3D clay |
| 📷 Camera | How close? From where? | close-up, far away, from above |
| ☀️ Light & color | What mood? | golden sunset, neon, pastel |
| 📐 Shape | Square, tall or wide? | square 1:1, tall 9:16, wide 16:9 |

```try

```
