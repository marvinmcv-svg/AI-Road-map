## 🔥 Warm-up: Which poster wins?
Look at 2 posters for 3 seconds each. What was each one about? Which one do you remember? Why?

## 💡 Big idea: the 4 design moves
1. 🥇 **Biggest first** — the most important thing is the biggest. Then the title. Then small details.
2. 🌗 **Contrast** — light on dark, or dark on light, so words are easy to read.
3. 📏 **Line it up and leave space** — crowded = ignored.
4. 🔤 **2 fonts at most** — one bold font for titles, one simple font for the rest.

**Call to action:** a short line that tells people what to do. Start with a doing word: *Try it free · Join the club · Order now*. It must be **honest**. No fake "only 2 left!"

## 🛠️ Your build: Launch Post + Poster
- [ ] Make a main picture in Gemini with empty space for words
- [ ] Social post (1:1 or 9:16): picture + title (7 words or less) + call to action + logo + #MadeWithAI
- [ ] Poster (4:5): picture + title + 3 short "why it's great" points + call to action + logo
- [ ] One thing is clearly the biggest

- [ ] Words are easy to read on the background
- [ ] Nothing untrue, and the AI label is there

## ⚔️ Challenge: The 3-Second Test
Show your poster to someone for 3 seconds. Hide it. Ask: *What's the brand? What does it sell? What should you do?* All 3 right? You win! If not, fix one thing and test again.

```group
Swap posters with different classmates.
```

```solo
Test your teacher first. Then find one more tester (a family member, or a video call). Then give your teacher's poster the 3-Second Test!
```

## 🎟️ Exit ticket
1. What does "biggest first" mean?
2. Write a call to action for your brand.
3. Why is "Only 1 left, buy now!" a problem if it isn't true?

## 🛡️ Stay safe
Ads must be honest. No fake reviews, no fake countdowns, no fake buyers.
