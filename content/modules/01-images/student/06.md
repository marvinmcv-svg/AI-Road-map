## 🔥 Warm-up: Which poster wins?
Look at two posters for 3 seconds each. Which one do you remember? Why?

## 💡 Big idea: 4 design moves
1. 🥇 **Hierarchy** — one thing is the biggest → headline → details → button
2. 🌗 **Contrast** — light text on dark, or dark on light
3. 📏 **Alignment & space** — line things up, leave empty space
4. 🔤 **Max 2 fonts** — one bold, one simple

**Call to action (CTA):** a short instruction that starts with a verb — *Try it free · Join the club · Pre-order now*. It must be **true**: no fake "only 1 left!" and no fake reviews.

📱 Stories (9:16): keep text away from the top and bottom — app buttons cover it!

## 🛠️ Your build
- [ ] Generate a hero image with empty space for text, in your brand colours
- [ ] **Social post** (1:1 or 9:16): image + headline (7 words max) + CTA + logo + #MadeWithAI
- [ ] **Poster** (4:5): image + headline + 3 short benefits + CTA + logo
- [ ] Check: one thing is clearly biggest
- [ ] Check: max 2 fonts, brand colours only
- [ ] Check: text is easy to read
- [ ] Check: no false claims

## ⚔️ Challenge: the 3-Second Test
Show your poster to a classmate for 3 seconds. Can they say the brand, the product and what to do? If not, fix ONE thing and try again!

## 🎟️ Exit ticket
1. What is visual hierarchy?
2. Write a CTA for your brand.
3. Why is "Only 1 left — buy now!" a problem if it isn't true?

## 🛡️ Stay safe
Honest ads build trust. Fake urgency and fake reviews are tricks — and sometimes illegal.

## 🚀 Bonus quest (+25 XP)
**A/B test:** make 2 versions with different headlines, ask 5 classmates which they prefer, and explain why it won.
