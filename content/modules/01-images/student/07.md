## 💡 Big idea: words + pictures
Your brain keeps words and pictures on two paths. When you learn with both, you have **two roads** to the memory. Then **quiz yourself**. That's the best way to study!

Three study helpers:
- 🃏 **Flashcard** — the word and a picture on the front. The meaning and an example on the back.
- 🔬 **Diagram** — a picture with labels and arrows.
- ✏️ **Picture notes** — one page with drawings, arrows and boxes.

**AI warning:** picture AI can make wrong labels and made-up words. So:
1. Get the facts from your textbook first.
2. Make the picture with **"no text"**.
3. Add the labels yourself, or check every label Gemini adds.
