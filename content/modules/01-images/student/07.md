## 💡 Big idea: dual coding
Your brain stores **words** and **pictures** on two roads. Use both = stronger memory! 🧠
- 🃏 **Flashcard:** term + picture on the front, definition + example on the back. Test yourself!
- 🏷️ **Labelled diagram:** a clear picture with arrows and labels
- ✏️ **Visual note:** one page with icons, boxes and arrows

⚠️ Image AI often gets labels wrong. The safe way:
1. Get the **facts first** from your textbook
2. Make the **picture with no text**
3. Add the **labels yourself**
