## 🔥 Warm-up
Name the 6 Image Prompt Formula parts. Then look at 3 photos. Where is the main thing? Why isn't it right in the middle?

## 💡 Big idea: the photographer's toolkit
- 🌄 **Far away** — shows the place
- 🧍 **Middle** — shows the action
- 🔍 **Close-up** — shows feelings and tiny details
- 🦅 **From above** — like a bird looking down
- ⬆️ **Looking up** — makes things look big and strong
- #️⃣ **Rule of thirds** — split the picture into a 3 × 3 grid. Put the main thing on a line. Leave **empty space** for words.

| Shape | Use it for |
|---|---|
| 1:1 (square) | posts, logos |
| 9:16 (tall) | phone stories |
| 16:9 (wide) | slides, video pictures |
| 4:5 (a bit tall) | posters |

## 🛠️ Your build: The Shot List
Pick ONE subject (like "a tiny dragon baking cupcakes"). Make each picture in **Gemini**.
- [ ] Plan all 4 prompts BEFORE you make any pictures
- [ ] Far away, 16:9
- [ ] Middle, 1:1
- [ ] Close-up, 4:5, the subject on the right with space for a title
- [ ] From above, 1:1
- [ ] Put all 4 in a grid with labels (Google Slides or Canva)

```try
A tiny green dragon baking cupcakes in a cozy kitchen, 3D clay style, seen from above, warm morning light, square 1:1.
```

## ⚔️ Challenge: Style Spinner
Spin for a style + a camera shot + a shape. Write a prompt that matches the spin. Make it in Gemini!

```group
Your house has 3 minutes per spin.
```

```solo
**You vs. your teacher.** You each spin and make a picture. Then guess the OTHER person's spin just by looking!
```

## 🎟️ Exit ticket
1. Name the camera shot in 3 small pictures.
2. Which shape is best for a phone story? For a slide?
3. What is empty space good for?
4. **Pick your company:** make up your Guild client. Write a name idea + what it sells + who buys it.

## 🛡️ Stay safe
Only take photos of things and places. Never photos of people without asking.
