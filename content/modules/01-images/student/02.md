## 🔥 Warm-up
Name the 6 Image Prompt Formula parts. Then look at 3 photos: where is the main subject? Why isn't it in the middle?

## 💡 Big idea: the photographer's toolkit
- 🌄 **Wide shot** — shows the place
- 🧍 **Medium shot** — shows the action
- 🔍 **Close-up** — shows feelings and details
- 🦅 **Bird's-eye view** — from above
- ⬆️ **Low angle** — makes things look powerful
- #️⃣ **Rule of thirds** — put the subject on the grid lines; leave **negative space** for text

| Shape | Use it for |
|---|---|
| 1:1 | feed posts, logos |
| 9:16 | phone stories |
| 16:9 | slides, thumbnails |
| 4:5 | posters |

## 🛠️ Your build: The Shot List
Pick ONE subject (like "a tiny dragon baking cupcakes").
- [ ] Plan all 4 prompts BEFORE generating
- [ ] Wide shot, 16:9
- [ ] Medium shot, 1:1
- [ ] Close-up, 4:5, subject on the right third with space for a title
- [ ] Bird's-eye view, 1:1
- [ ] Put all 4 in a Canva grid with labels

## ⚔️ Challenge: Style Roulette
Spin for a style + shot + shape. Your house has 3 minutes to write a prompt that matches the spin!

## 🎟️ Exit ticket
1. Label 3 mini images with their shot type.
2. Which shape for a phone story? For a slide?
3. What is negative space useful for?
4. **Startup pick:** invent your Guild client — name idea + product + customers.

## 🛡️ Stay safe
Only photograph objects and places — never people without permission.
