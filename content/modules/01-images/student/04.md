## 🔥 Warm-up: Real or Edited?
Look at 6 pictures. Real or edited? Point to the clue: shadows, edges or patterns that repeat.

## 💡 Big idea: the 4 AI edits
In Gemini, you edit by **asking for a change in the same chat**.
- ✂️ **Remove background** — "Put it on a plain white background."
- 🖌️ **Swap one thing** — "Take away the stain." / "Add a small plant."
- ↔️ **Expand** — "Make it wide, 16:9, with empty space on the left."
- 🔍 **Upscale** — "Make it sharper and more clear."

**Honest:** clean the background, fix the light, put the product in a new place, **and add a label**.
**Tricky:** make it look bigger or better than it is, fake results, fake buyers.

## 🎟️ Exit ticket

3. Why do we never edit photos of real people?

## 🛡️ Stay safe
We edit **things, places and made-up characters**. Never real people. Changing someone's face or body without a "yes" is a deepfake. It can really hurt.
