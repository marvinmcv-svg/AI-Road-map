## 🔥 Warm-up: Real or Edited?
Vote on 6 images. What clues gave them away? (shadows, edges, repeated patterns)

## 💡 Big idea: the 4 AI edits
- ✂️ **Background removal** — cut out the subject
- 🖌️ **Inpainting / generative fill** — change one part inside the picture
- ↔️ **Outpainting / expand** — make the picture bigger at the edges
- 🔎 **Upscaling** — make it bigger and sharper

**Honest edit:** clean background, better light, new scene + label ✅
**Misleading edit:** product looks bigger or better than it really is, fake results, fake customers ❌

## 🛠️ Your build: Product Photo Glow-Up
Photograph one object (no people!) at the photo corner, then:
- [ ] Remove the background → `product-cutout`
- [ ] Put it in a new scene that fits your brand → `product-scene`
- [ ] Use generative fill to add or remove one detail → `product-fill`
- [ ] Expand it into a 16:9 banner with space for text → `product-banner`
- [ ] Add an "AI-edited" label
- [ ] Write: "This edit is honest because…"

## ⚔️ Challenge: Ad Court
Be the judge! For each ad: **Honest, Misleading, or Needs a label?** Explain why.

## 🎟️ Exit ticket
1. Match: inpainting / outpainting / upscaling / background removal → what each does.
2. Give one honest and one misleading edit.
3. Why do we never edit photos of real people in class?

## 🛡️ Stay safe
Editing someone's face or body without permission can hurt them — that's how deepfakes start. In this class we edit **objects, places and AI characters only**.
