## 🔥 Warm-up: Two Truths and a Hallucination
Your teacher shows 3 AI "facts". One is made up! Which one? And **how could you check**?

## 💡 Big idea
AI can get things wrong in 3 ways:
- 🌀 **Hallucination** — it sounds sure, but it's false. *Fix:* the **Two-Source Rule**. Check 2 trusted places.
- ⚖️ **Bias** — it copies unfair patterns from its examples. *Fix:* notice it, and ask for more kinds of people.
- 📅 **Out of date** — it doesn't know new news. *Fix:* check the date on your source.

**Rules of the AI road:**
- 🎂 Follow the age rules.
- 🤝 No photos of other people without a "yes".
- 🔒 Keep private things private.
- 🏷️ Say when you used AI (#MadeWithAI).

## 🛡️ Stay safe
A **deepfake** is a fake picture, video or voice of a real person. Even as a "joke" it can really hurt someone. It can be against the law too.
