## 🔥 Warm-up: Two Truths and a Hallucination
Your teacher shows 3 AI "facts". One is made up! Vote — and say **how you could check**.

## 💡 Big idea
AI can go wrong in 3 ways:
- 🌀 **Hallucination** — sounds sure, but it's false. *Fix:* the **Two-Source Rule** (check 2 trusted sources).
- ⚖️ **Bias** — repeats unfair patterns from its training data. *Fix:* notice it and ask for variety.
- 📅 **Out of date** — doesn't know recent news. *Fix:* check the date of your source.

**Rules of the AI road:** 🎂 follow age rules · 🤝 no photos of others without permission · 🔒 keep private info private · 🏷️ be honest when you use AI (#MadeWithAI).

## 🛡️ Stay safe
A **deepfake** is fake media of a real person. Even as a "joke" it can really hurt someone — and it can be against the law.
