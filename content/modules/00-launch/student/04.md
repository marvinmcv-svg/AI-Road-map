## 🔥 Warm-up: Recall Raid
Your house answers 5 quick questions about lessons 1–3. Get 4 right for XP!

## 💡 Duel rules
- Every house gets the same **target**.
- Write a prompt → run it → compare → improve.
- 🎯 Hit the target: **+30** · 🔁 each extra try: **−3** · 📋 all 5 recipe parts labelled: **+10** · 🔎 catch a hallucination: **+10**
- No prompt log = no points!

## ⚔️ The Duel
- [ ] Round 1: text target
- [ ] Round 2: table target (check 2 numbers with a real source!)
- [ ] Round 3: image target
- [ ] Prompt log complete for every round

## 🛠️ Your build: Guild nickname + avatar
- [ ] Pick a Guild nickname (no real name, nothing personal)
- [ ] Write an avatar prompt: a character, animal or object — **not your face**
- [ ] Add 2 things you love and your house colour
- [ ] Generate it (or give your prompt to your teacher)
- [ ] Improve it once and log what changed

```try
Create an avatar for a game profile: a pixel-art panda wearing headphones and holding a skateboard, pink house colors, square, centered, plain background.
```

## 🎟️ Exit ticket
1. Which prompt change helped your house most?
2. Rate your prompting skill 1–5. What will you practise?
3. One thing you're excited to create this year.

## 🛡️ Stay safe
Avatars protect you online — no selfies. And no famous characters (like superheroes): make an original!

## 🚀 Level-up (+50 XP)
Write a prompt that turns the AI into a quiz-master for our vocabulary. Test it on a classmate and improve it twice.
