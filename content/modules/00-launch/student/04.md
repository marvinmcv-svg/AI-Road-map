## 🔥 Warm-up: Recall Raid
Answer 5 quick questions about lessons 1 to 3. Get 4 right for XP!

## 💡 Duel rules
- Every player gets the same **target**.
- Write a prompt → run it in Gemini → compare → make it better.
- 🎯 Hit the target: **+30** · 🔁 each extra try: **−3** · 📋 all 5 recipe parts labeled: **+10** · 🔎 catch a hallucination: **+10**
- No Prompt Log = no points!

```group
You play for your house against the other houses.
```

```solo
You play against your teacher. Same targets, same rules. May the best prompt win!
```

## ⚔️ The Duel
- [ ] Round 1: text target
- [ ] Round 2: table target (check 2 numbers with a real source!)
- [ ] Round 3: picture target
- [ ] Prompt Log done for every round

## 🛠️ Your build: Guild nickname + avatar
- [ ] Pick a Guild nickname. No real name, nothing private.
- [ ] Write an avatar prompt: a character, animal or object. **Not your face.**
- [ ] Add 2 things you love and your house color
- [ ] Make it in Gemini (or give your prompt to your teacher)
- [ ] Ask for one change. Write down what changed.

```try
Make an avatar for a game profile: a pixel-art panda wearing headphones and holding a skateboard, pink colors, square, in the middle, plain background.
```

## 🎟️ Exit ticket
1. Which prompt change helped you most?
2. How good are you at prompts, from 1 to 5? What will you practice?
3. What is one thing you're excited to make this year?

## 🛡️ Stay safe
Avatars keep you safe online. No selfies! And no famous characters (like superheroes). Make your own!

## 🚀 Level-up (+50 XP)
Write a prompt that turns Gemini into a quiz show host for our new words. Test it on someone. Make it better 2 times.
