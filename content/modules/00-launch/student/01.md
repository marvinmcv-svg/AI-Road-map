## 🔥 Warm-up
Open **Quick, Draw!** with your partner and doodle 6 things. Then talk: *How do you think the computer guessed your drawing? Where did it learn?*

## 💡 Big idea
> **AI doesn't *know* things. It predicts from patterns.**

AI learns from tons of examples called **training data**. It finds **patterns** (cats have pointy ears!) and uses them to make a **prediction** about something new.

## 🛠️ Your build
Open **Teachable Machine → Image Project**. Point the webcam at **objects only** (not faces!).
- [ ] Class 1: record a pencil from lots of angles (about 50 pictures)
- [ ] Class 2: record an eraser the same way
- [ ] Class 3: record "nothing" (empty table)
- [ ] Click **Train Model**
- [ ] Test it 4 ways: normal, upside down, a pen it never saw, in dim light
- [ ] Fill in your Training Data Detective sheet
- [ ] Finish this sentence: "Our model was wrong when ___ because the training data ___."

## ⚔️ Challenge: Break the Model!
You have 5 minutes. Make your model **super sure about the WRONG answer** (90%+). Then explain your trick using the words *training data*. Most creative trick wins house XP!

## 🎟️ Exit ticket
1. What is **training data**? (your own words)
2. Why did your model make a mistake? Use the word *pattern*.
3. One question you have about AI.

## 🛡️ Stay safe
Pictures of people's faces are personal. We point cameras at **objects**, never at classmates.

## 🚀 Bonus quest (+25 XP)
**Glitch Hunt:** find the weirdest way to fool your model and explain why it works.
