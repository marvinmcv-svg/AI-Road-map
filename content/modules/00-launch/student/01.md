## 🔥 Warm-up
Open **Quick, Draw!** and doodle 6 things. Then think: *How did the computer guess your drawing? Where did it learn that?*

## 💡 Big idea
> **AI doesn't *know* things. It guesses from patterns.**

AI learns from lots and lots of examples. We call them **training data**. It looks for **patterns** (cats have pointy ears!). Then it makes a **prediction**. That's a fancy word for its best guess.

## 🛠️ Your build
Open **Teachable Machine → Image Project**. Point the webcam at **things only**. No faces!
- [ ] Class 1: take about 50 pictures of a pencil. Turn it lots of ways.
- [ ] Class 2: do the same with an eraser
- [ ] Class 3: take pictures of "nothing" (the empty table)
- [ ] Click **Train Model**
- [ ] Test it 4 ways: normal, upside down, a pen it never saw, and in low light
- [ ] Fill in your Training Data Detective sheet
- [ ] Finish this: "My AI was wrong when ___ because the training data ___."

## ⚔️ Challenge: Break the Model!
Make your AI **super sure about the WRONG answer** (90% or more). Then explain your trick. Use the words *training data*.

```group
You have 5 minutes with your partner. The best trick wins house XP!
```

```solo
**You vs. your teacher.** You each get 3 tries to fool the AI. Who can trick it the most?
```

## 🎟️ Exit ticket
1. What is **training data**? Use your own words.
2. Why did your AI make a mistake? Use the word *pattern*.
3. What is one question you have about AI?
