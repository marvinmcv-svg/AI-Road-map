## 🔥 Warm-up
**Be the autocomplete!** Write the next word: "Once upon a…", "Peanut butter and…", "My favourite colour is…". When did everyone agree? When didn't they?

## 💡 Big idea
AI writes one small piece (a **token**) at a time, guessing what comes next. **Details help it guess better.** Use the Guild Prompt Recipe:

| Part | Ask yourself |
|---|---|
| 🎭 **Role** | Who should the AI act as? |
| 🎯 **Task** | What exactly should it do? |
| 🔍 **Details** | What must it include or avoid? |
| 🖌️ **Style** | How should it sound or look? |
| 📐 **Format** | What shape is the answer? (list, table, poem…) |

```try
You are a friendly science teacher. Explain how volcanoes erupt, mention magma and pressure, in no more than 5 sentences, for 12-year-olds, with one joke. Give it as a numbered list, then a 1-question quiz.
```

## 🛠️ Your build: the Prompt Ladder
Pick a mission card (snack recipe 🍕, study guide 📚, playground game 🎮, dragon story 🐉).
- [ ] v1: write a one-line prompt and save the answer
- [ ] v2: add **Role + Task**
- [ ] v3: add **Details + Style**
- [ ] v4: add **Format**
- [ ] For every version, write what you changed and **why**
- [ ] Check one fact in your final answer (is it true? is it safe?)

## ⚔️ Challenge: Prompt Telephone
Your teacher shows a mystery AI answer. Your house has 4 minutes to write the prompt that made it. Closest match wins!

## 🎟️ Exit ticket
1. Name the 5 parts of the Prompt Recipe — from memory!
2. Why do details give better results?
3. Fix this weak prompt: "write about dogs".

## 🛡️ Stay safe
Never put your full name, school, address or passwords in a prompt. Say "a 12-year-old student" instead of your name.
