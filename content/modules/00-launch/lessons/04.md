## Lesson objectives
By the end, the student can…
- Use the whole Prompt Recipe to hit a target in as few tries as they can.
- Write down each try and say why they changed it.
- Check at least one AI fact and spot any hallucination.
- Write a safe avatar prompt that is not their real face.

## Words for today
All the Launch words. Most of all: prompt, iteration, hallucination, details, format.

## 1. Warm-up — Recall Raid: Season 1 review (10 min · 1-on-1: 5 min)
```teacher
Today is Boss day! First, a fast quiz on what we learned. Then you will duel me with prompts. Best prompt wins. Ready?
```

1. What does an AI model learn from?
2. Name the 5 parts of the Prompt Recipe.
3. What's a hallucination? How do you catch one?
4. Safe or Sorry: "Turn a classmate's photo into a meme without asking."
5. Why can two people get different answers from the same prompt?

```group
Houses answer on whiteboards. +10 XP per house member for every house with 4 or more right.
```

```solo
Rapid fire, out loud. 4 or more right = +10 XP. Missed one? Come back to it at the end of the lesson.
```

## 2. Mini-lesson — Duel rules (5 min)
Show **Visual 1 (Duel arena)**. Rules:
- Each round has a **target card**. It shows what the final answer must look like.
- Write a prompt → run it in Gemini → compare it to the target → fix it and try again.
- **Score:** 30 points for hitting the target. Lose 3 points for every extra try after the first. +10 if every recipe part is labeled on the score sheet. +10 for catching a hallucination.
- You must keep a Prompt Log. No log, no points.

## 4. Game / challenge — THE PROMPT DUEL (35 min · 1-on-1: 25 min)
*(Today the Boss comes first. The fun build is the reward.)*

**Round 1 — Text target:** "A 4-line rhyming poem about homework. Each line starts with a letter of the word 'STOP'. It ends with '!'"

**Round 2 — Table target:** "A table with 3 columns (Planet, How far from the Sun in million km, Fun fact) for the 4 planets closest to the Sun, closest first." ➜ Check **two numbers** with a trusted source. +10 if you catch a mistake.

**Round 3 — Picture target:** show the target picture you made earlier. For example: *"a pixel-art red fox wearing an astronaut helmet, floating above a purple planet, square"*. Write a prompt to make it again, as close as you can. Make it in Gemini.

Everyone who finishes the Prompt Log earns the **⚔️ Prompt Duelist** badge.

```group
Houses play the same rounds. You make each house's picture on the big screen and the class votes for the closest. Winning house +15 XP each, every house that finishes +5. Write the scores in the tracker.
```

```solo
**Student vs. Bolt.** You and the student both play every round, each on your own Gemini screen (or take turns on one). Keep score on the sheet. For Round 3, the student judges your picture and you judge theirs. Both must say why. If the student wins, +15 XP. If they finish, +5. Play fair, but don't make it too easy.
```

## 3. Hands-on build — Guild Nickname & Avatar (25 min · 1-on-1: 15 min)
1. Pick a **Guild nickname**. No real name, no school, nothing private. For example "PixelPanda" or "NovaByte".
2. Write an avatar prompt with the recipe. It must be a **character, animal or object. Not your real face.**
   - Role/Task: "Make an avatar for a game profile…"
   - Details: a favorite animal or object, 2 things you love, your house color.
   - Style: pixel art, 3D clay, cartoon or watercolor. No copied characters or brands.
   - Format: square, in the middle, plain background.
3. Make it in **Gemini**.
4. Ask Gemini for one change (like "make the background blue"). Write down what changed.

**Checkpoint (+20 XP):** nickname + avatar + prompt saved in the portfolio folder.

```solo
Let the student lead. Ask "What do you love doing?" to fill the Details. If they want lots of tries, that's fine. Count each one in the log. Help them save it.
```

> 🛡️ **Safety & copying moment:** Why not upload a selfie? Your face is private. An avatar keeps you safe online. Why not "make me Spider-Man"? Characters belong to companies. We make our own.

## 5. Share-out — Guild Hall reveal (10 min · 1-on-1: 5 min)
Show the avatars with their nicknames (only if the student wants to). Say who won the Duel. Hand out badges in the tracker. Then tease Module 1: *"A new company wants the Guild to make its whole look…"*

```group
Slideshow of avatars. Update the house leaderboard.
```

```solo
Set the avatar as their Guild card picture. The student tells you the story of their avatar in 3 sentences. Award the badge together.
```

## 6. Exit ticket (5 min)
1. Which change to your prompt helped most today? Why?
2. How good are you at prompts, 1 to 5? What will you practice next?
3. What is one thing you're excited to make this year?

```solo
Talk it through. Write their 1 to 5 score in your notes, so you can see it grow later.
```

## Differentiation
- **Extra help:** give a prompt starter for each round. Let them use the Recipe card. Do Round 3 together.
- **Extra challenge:** hit the target in ONE try. Or make the avatar in 3 styles and pick the best.
- **1-on-1 tip:** if the student is shy, let them be "the judge" in Round 1 first, then a player.
