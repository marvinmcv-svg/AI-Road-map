## 🔥 Warm-up: The leaky app
Find 4 things in your teacher's "leaky" app that could hurt users.

## 💡 Big idea: App Trust Checklist
1. 🎯 **Only what's needed** — remove fields you don't use
2. 🗑️ **Delete my data** — one button, everything gone
3. 📄 **Privacy note** — what, why, who sees it, how to delete
4. 🔐 **Locked down** — RLS on, keys on the server, 2-step login
5. 🤖 **Safe AI** — no medical, legal or money advice; point to real help
6. ♿ **For everyone** — screen reader, big text, high contrast

```try
Write a short privacy note in plain words for my app. It collects: email (for login), date and number of glasses of water, and an optional note. Say why we collect it, who can see it, and how to delete it.
```
