## 🔥 Warm-up: The forgetful app
Add something to your app. Close it completely. Open it again. Is it still there?

## 💡 Big idea: data is a table
| Item | Fields |
|---|---|
| Water log | date · glasses · note |
| Expense | date · amount · category |

**CRUD:** ➕ Create (add) · 👀 Read (show) · ✏️ Update (edit) · 🗑️ Delete

**Local storage:** saves on THIS phone. Fast and private, but not on your other devices (yet!).

```try
Make my app save real data on the device (local storage) using this data model: [paste]. Make add, show, edit and delete work on these screens: [list]. Remove the fake sample data. Show a friendly message when there's no data yet. Explain how the saving works in simple words.
```
