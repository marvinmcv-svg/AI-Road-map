## 🔥 Warm-up: Guess the feedback
What will testers love? What will they complain about? Write it down and fold the paper.

## 💡 Big idea: the beta test plan
1. 📨 **Invite** 3 or more testers (send the link + how to install)
2. ✅ **Give tasks** — "Use it for 3 days to…"
3. 📝 **Survey** — 5 short questions
4. 📊 **Count** — how many entries? (counts only, never private entries)
5. 🎯 **Sort and pick** — themes → impact vs. effort → top 3 fixes

```try
Here is the feedback from my app's beta testers (no names): [paste]. Sort it into themes. What are the 3 biggest problems, and how could each be fixed?
```

## 🛠️ Your build
- [ ] Send the app to 3 or more testers
- [ ] Collect survey answers
- [ ] Sort the feedback into themes with Claude
- [ ] Place fixes on the impact vs. effort grid
- [ ] Ship the top 3 fixes as version 2
- [ ] Write release notes and send them to testers

## ⚔️ Challenge: Impact vs. Effort
Place 8 fixes on the grid in 3 minutes. Which 2 would you do first? Defend it!

```group
The class votes on the best-defended choices.
```

```solo
**You vs. your teacher.** Place the same 8 fixes secretly, then compare and debate!
```

## 🎟️ Exit ticket
1. What's a beta test for?
2. What is the impact vs. effort grid?
3. What did testers love that you didn't expect?

## 🛡️ Stay safe
Testers' data is real. Look only at counts and survey answers.
