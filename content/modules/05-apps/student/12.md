## 🔥 Warm-up: Word Lightning Round
Your teacher shows a word. Say what it means and give an example. Fast!

## 💡 What "Pro" looks like
Look at the rubric with your teacher, one row at a time.

## 🛠️ Your build: Launch checklist
- [ ] Installs on a phone and opens from the home screen
- [ ] Every user story works in 3 taps or fewer
- [ ] Cloud data with login, RLS on, peek test passed
- [ ] AI feature works, key only on the server, limits on
- [ ] All 5 app states feel good
- [ ] App Trust Checklist passes
- [ ] Test checklist passes after the last change
- [ ] Version 2 shipped with release notes
- [ ] Score yourself with the rubric and fix your lowest row

## ⚔️ Challenge: App Launch Showcase
Your 4-minute demo from the home screen:
1. The problem ("For ___, who ___…")
2. Log in and do the main user story
3. Show the AI feature, and say where the key lives
4. Show the privacy note and the delete button
5. "Testers said ___, so in version 2 I ___."
6. Honest limits ("It can't ___ yet.")

```group
Every guest installs the app and gives one ⭐ strength and one ❓ question.
```

```solo
Your teacher and a guest install your app and review it like an app store: 1 to 5 stars and a one-line review!
```

## 🎟️ Exit ticket
1. What part of your app are you most proud of? Why?
2. What did beta testing teach you?
3. What would you build next?

## 🛡️ Stay safe
Decide what to keep running after the launch, and turn off what you don't need.

## 🚀 Level-up (+50 XP)
Ship version 3 with offline mode or data export, and get one real person to use it for a week. (Sneak peek: next up is **Games**! 🕹️)
