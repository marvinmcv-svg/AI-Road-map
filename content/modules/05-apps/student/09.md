## 🔥 Warm-up: The cold app
Use your teacher's "bad feel" app on a slow network. Where did you think it was broken?

## 💡 Big idea: the 5 app states
| State | Good example |
|---|---|
| ⏳ Loading | "Loading your week…" |
| 🫙 Empty | "No entries yet. Tap + to log your first glass!" |
| ⚠️ Error | "We couldn't save that. Check your internet and try again." + Retry |
| ✅ Success | "Saved!" with a check |
| 👋 First time | 2 or 3 short welcome screens |

**Good messages:** what happened · why · what to do next. Never blame the user.

```try
Write friendly messages for my water tracker app for these states: loading, empty, error (no internet) and success. Short, warm and clear, in a fun but calm voice.
```
