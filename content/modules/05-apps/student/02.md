## 🔥 Warm-up: Use a paper app
Tap the paper "buttons" while your teacher swaps the screens. Where did you get stuck?

## 💡 Big idea: stories → screens → flow
- 📝 **User story:** "As a ___, I want to ___, so that ___."
- 📱 **Screens:** Home · Add · History · Settings
- ➡️ **User flow:** the path through the screens to get one thing done

**Rules for version 1:**
- 3 to 5 user stories. Extra ideas go on a **Later** list.
- Every story takes **3 taps or fewer**.
- A **tab bar** at the bottom with 2 to 4 tabs.

```try
Here are my user stories and screens: [paste]. Which story has no screen? Which flow takes more than 3 taps? What would confuse a new user?
```

## ⚔️ Challenge: Paper App Test
A tester tries a task on your paper app. You play the computer. Don't explain anything!

```group
Test with someone from another house.
```

```solo
**You vs. your teacher.** Test each other's paper apps. Fix one thing and test again!
```

## 🎟️ Exit ticket
1. Write one user story for your app.
2. What's a user flow?
3. Why test on paper before building?

## 🛡️ Stay safe
Look at every screen: does it ask for anything private you don't really need?

## 🚀 Bonus quest (+25 XP)
Add one "edge case" flow: what happens when there's no data yet, or the user makes a mistake?
