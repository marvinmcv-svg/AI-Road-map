## 🔥 Warm-up: My phone, my problems
Pick 3 apps you use a lot. What problem does each one solve? What problem do YOU have that no app solves well?
📣 **New quest:** build your own app, just for you!

## 💡 Big idea: 3 ways to make an app
| | Native app | Web app | Installable web app ✅ |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lives in | App Store / Google Play | A web link | A web link + your home screen |
| Works on | iPhone OR Android | Any browser | Any phone or computer |
| Share it | Store review | Send a link | Send a link, then "Add to Home Screen" |

We'll build an **installable web app** with Claude. One build works on every phone!

**Good ideas are small:** not "a fitness app", but "log my water in 10 seconds, with a streak."

**Boss options:** 📊 tracker · 📔 diary · 🎓 AI tutor · 🗓️ scheduler · or your own idea!

```try
Make a simple mobile app prototype as an artifact for this idea: a college student logs how many glasses of water they drink and sees a streak. Just 2 or 3 screens, big buttons, a bottom tab bar. Use fake sample data. Keep it simple.
```
