## 🔥 Warm-up: Easy or annoying?
Look at 4 apps. Which are easy to use? Which are annoying? Point to why.

## 💡 Big idea: 6 rules for thumbs
1. 👍 **Thumb zone** — main buttons at the bottom
2. 🧭 **Tab bar** — 2 to 4 tabs, with icons AND words
3. 🔘 **Big tap targets** — fingertip size, with space between
4. 📋 **Simple lists** — most important info first
5. ✍️ **Short forms** — buttons and pickers instead of typing
6. ✅ **Feedback** — every tap shows something happened

**Style guide:** 2 colors · 1 font · 1 button style · your icon

```try
Here are photos of my paper app screens and my style guide: [colors, font, button style]. Make a polished, clickable mobile app design as an artifact. Main buttons in the thumb zone, a bottom tab bar with icons and words, big tap targets, feedback after every tap. Use fake sample data.
```

## ⚔️ Challenge: One-Thumb Test
Hold the phone in one hand. Do the main task with just your thumb. Count the stretches!

```group
Fewest stretches wins.
```


## 🎟️ Exit ticket

2. Name 4 of the 6 rules.
