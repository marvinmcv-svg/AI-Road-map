## Lesson objectives
By the end, the student can…
- Demo a working, installable app from the home screen.
- Explain how it saves data safely, how the AI feature works, and what testing changed.
- Score their app with the rubric and fix the weakest part.

## Words for today
All the Module 5 words. Most of all: user story, user flow, installable web app, CRUD, database, Row Level Security, server function, app states, beta test.

```group
First house to answer scores.
```

```solo
Time it: how many words in 2 minutes? Try to beat your Module 4 score.
```

## 2. Mini-lesson — What "Pro" looks like (5 min)
Go through the rubric's **4 · Pro** column, one row at a time. Show a strong example (your demo app).

## 3. Hands-on build — Launch checklist (35 min · 1-on-1: 25 min)
- [ ] Installs on a phone and opens from the home screen
- [ ] Every user story works in 3 taps or fewer
- [ ] Data saves to the cloud, per user, with login (RLS on, peek test passed)
- [ ] AI feature works, key only on the server, login check and daily limit on
- [ ] Loading, empty, error, success and first-time states feel good
- [ ] App Trust Checklist passes (only what's needed, delete my data, privacy note, safe AI, works for everyone)
- [ ] Test checklist passes after the last change
- [ ] Version 2 shipped with release notes from beta feedback

Score yourself with the rubric. Write one sentence of proof for each row. Last 10 minutes: fix the lowest row. Deploy.
**Checkpoint (+20 XP):** checklist done + self-score written.

```solo
Let the student self-score first, then compare with your score. Where you disagree, open the app and look together.
```

## 4. Game / challenge — App Launch Showcase (30 min · 1-on-1: 15 min)
A 4-minute live demo from the home screen:
1. **The problem:** "For [who], who [problem]…"
2. **The app:** open it from the home screen, log in, do the main user story.
3. **The AI feature:** show it helping, and say where the key lives.
4. **Trust:** show the privacy note and the delete button.
5. **Testing:** "Testers said ___, so in version 2 I ___."
6. **Honest limits:** "It can't ___ yet. Next, I'd add ___."

Guests try the app on their own phones and give one ⭐ strength and one ❓ question.

**Boss XP:** rubric total (4 rows × 1 to 4 points) × 6 = up to 96. Round up to 100 for a perfect score. **📱 App Engineer** badge for 3 or more on every row.

```group
Awards: "Most Useful App", "Smoothest Design", "Smartest AI Feature" (+15 house XP each).
```

```solo
**App store day.** Guests act like app store reviewers: each one installs the app on their phone, uses it for 2 minutes, then gives it 1 to 5 stars and a one-line review. Award "Best in Show" (+15 XP).
```

## 5. Share-out — Launch party (10 min · 1-on-1: 5 min)
Celebrate! Then tease the next quest: *"You've built a brand, a store, robot helpers and your own app. The final quest: design and ship your own GAME."*

```solo
Ask the student if they'll keep using their app. Help them decide what to keep running and what to turn off (Supabase project, API spending limit).
```

## 6. Exit ticket (5 min)
1. What part of your app are you most proud of? Why?
2. What did beta testing teach you?
3. What would you build next with one more lesson?

```solo
Ask out loud. Save the answers for a progress note.
```

## Differentiation
- **Extra help:** an app with login, cloud saving and the trust checklist (no AI feature) can still reach level 3 on most rows.
- **Extra challenge:** add a home screen badge or reminder, a share feature, or a second AI feature, and test it with a beta tester.
- **1-on-1 tip:** real users make this the best day of the module. Even one guest installing the app on their own phone is a big deal.
