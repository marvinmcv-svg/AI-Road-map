## Lesson objectives
By the end, the student can…
- Use the **App Trust Checklist** on their own app and fix what's missing.
- Add a "delete my data" option and a short, true privacy note.
- Make the AI feature safe for real people, and check the app with a screen reader and large text.

## Words for today
- **Data minimization** — only collecting the data your app really needs.
- **Consent** (again) — asking clearly before collecting or sharing something.
- **Privacy note** — a short page that says what you collect, why, and how to delete it.
- **Accessible** (again) — easy to use for everyone, including people with disabilities.
- **Guardrails** (again) — rules that keep the AI feature from doing harm.

## 1. Warm-up — The leaky app (10 min · 1-on-1: 5 min)
Use the "leaky" demo app. Find 4 things that could hurt users or break their trust.

```solo
The student finds the 4 problems. Then ask: "Which one would make YOU delete the app?"
```

## 2. Mini-lesson — The App Trust Checklist (15 min · 1-on-1: 10 min)
Show **Visual 1**:
1. 🎯 **Only what's needed:** remove every field your user stories don't use.
2. 🗑️ **Delete my data:** a button that deletes the account and all their data.
3. 📄 **Privacy note:** what you collect, why, who can see it, how to delete it. Only true lines.
4. 🔐 **Locked down:** RLS on (lesson 7), keys only on the server (lesson 8), 2-step login on your own accounts.
5. 🤖 **Safe AI:** clear rules; no medical, legal or money advice; gentle "please talk to a trusted person or professional" when someone seems in trouble; never pretend to be a human.
6. ♿ **For everyone:** works with a screen reader, big text and high contrast; every button has a clear name.

```teacher
People will put real parts of their life into your app: their habits, money, moods. That's trust. The best apps protect it like it's their own.
```

## 3. Hands-on build — Make it trustworthy (35 min · 1-on-1: 25 min)
1. Go through the checklist on your app. Mark each item ✅ or ❌.
2. Remove any field you don't need.
3. Ask Claude for a plain-words privacy note from your real data model. Cut every line that isn't true.
4. Ask Claude Code:
```
Add to my app: (1) a Privacy page with this note: [paste], linked from Settings; (2) a "Delete my account and data" button in Settings that asks "Are you sure?" and then deletes everything for that user; (3) clear names on every button for screen readers, and support for the phone's large text setting.
Also update the AI feature's system prompt with these safety rules: [paste]. Explain each change simply.
```
5. Test: delete a test account; check its data is gone in Supabase. Run the test checklist again.

**Checkpoint 1 (+20 XP):** checklist done; unneeded fields removed; privacy note true and live.
**Checkpoint 2 (+20 XP):** delete-my-data works; AI safety rules added; screen reader test passed.

```group
Pairs check each other's privacy notes: is every line true for that app?
```

```solo
Read the privacy note together line by line: "Do we really do this?" Then test the delete button on a test account together.
```

> 🛡️ **Real help moment:** if your app is about moods or wellness, add a "Need to talk to someone?" link to real support lines in your country.

## 4. Game / challenge — Screen Reader Mission (15 min · 1-on-1: 10 min)
Turn on the phone's screen reader. With eyes closed, do your app's main task. Then turn on the biggest text size. Does anything break?

```group
Every app that passes both: +15 XP. Everyone who fixes a problem: +5.
```

```solo
**Student vs. Bolt.** You do the screen reader mission on their app; they do it on your demo. Time it. Fix one thing and try again. +15 XP for playing, +5 for a faster second try.
```

## 5. Share-out — The Trust Promise (10 min · 1-on-1: 5 min)
Add a line to your Safety Pact: "When I build apps, I promise to…" Read it out loud.

```solo
The student reads their promise. Add it to their pact in the portfolio.
```

## 6. Exit ticket (5 min)
1. What is data minimization? Give an example from your app.
2. Name 3 safety rules for your AI feature.
3. How did you test that your app works for everyone?

```solo
Ask out loud. Next lesson, real people try the app.
```

## Differentiation
- **Extra help:** focus on 3 items: only what's needed, delete my data, privacy note.
- **Extra challenge:** let users download all their data as a file (data export), and add an "AI off" switch in Settings.
- **1-on-1 tip:** ask the student what makes them trust (or not trust) an app with their own data. Use their answers as the checklist's "why".
