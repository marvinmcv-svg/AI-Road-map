## Lesson objectives
By the end, the student can…
- Launch a finished, original game with a real link.
- Pitch it in 3 minutes, explaining design choices with game words.
- Score their game with the rubric and fix the weakest part.

## Words for today
All the Module 6 words. Most of all: core loop, mechanic, game loop, game juice, difficulty curve, balance, leaderboard, fallback, playtest, accessible.

---

## 1. Warm-up — Word lightning round (5 min)
```teacher
Arcade day! Everyone plays your game. Be proud. You made a real game. Fast word game first.
```

Show a word. Say what it means and give an example. 10 words, fast.

```group
First house to answer scores.
```

```solo
Time it: how many words in 2 minutes? Try to beat your Module 5 score.
```

## 2. Mini-lesson — What "Pro" looks like (5 min)
Go through the rubric's **4 · Pro** column, one row at a time. Show a strong example (your demo game).

## 3. Hands-on build — Launch checklist (30 min · 1-on-1: 20 min)
- [ ] Original name, characters, art and sounds, with a Credits screen
- [ ] Core loop is fun in the first 30 seconds
- [ ] Start, pause, game over and play again all work
- [ ] 3+ levels or waves that teach → practice → test
- [ ] Balanced: tuned with real playtest numbers
- [ ] High score + leaderboard with nickname filter, score limits and RLS
- [ ] AI feature (if any): key on the server, limits, fallback lines
- [ ] Fair & Fun Checklist passes (access, fair, kind)
- [ ] Works on phone and computer, from the live link

Score yourself with the rubric. Write one sentence of proof for each row. Last 10 minutes: fix the lowest row. Deploy.
**Checkpoint (+20 XP):** checklist done + self-score written.

```solo
Let the student self-score first, then compare with your score. Where you disagree, play that part together.
```

## 4. Game / challenge — Game Launch Arcade (35 min · 1-on-1: 20 min)
**The 3-minute pitch** (like a game trailer):
1. **Hook:** show the best 20 seconds of play.
2. **The idea:** "It's a ___ where you ___ to ___."
3. **Design story:** "Playtesters struggled with ___, so I changed ___."
4. **Behind the scenes:** "Claude Code built ___. I designed ___. The AI character does ___."
5. **Play it:** share the link!

Then guests play on their own devices and leave a ⭐ strength, a ❓ question and their high score.

**Boss XP:** rubric total (4 rows × 1 to 4 points) × 6 = up to 96. Round up to 100 for a perfect score. **🕹️ Game Designer** badge for 3 or more on every row.

```group
Awards: "Most Fun", "Best Twist on a Classic Type", "Best Game Feel", "Most Welcoming Game" (+15 house XP each).
```

```solo
**Launch night.** Count down "3, 2, 1, launch!" and share the link with every guest at once. Watch the leaderboard fill up live. Award "Best in Show" to their best moment (+15 XP).
```

## 5. Share-out — Arcade party (10 min · 1-on-1: 5 min)
Celebrate! Then look ahead: *"Next, Demo Day: you'll show the world your whole year: your brand, video, store, robot helpers, app and game."*

```solo
Ask the student which project of the year they're proudest of. That's the star of their Demo Day.
```

## 6. Exit ticket (5 min)
1. What part of your game are you most proud of? Why?
2. What did playtesting teach you?
3. What would you add with one more week?

```solo
Ask out loud. Save the answers for the Demo Day portfolio.
```

## Differentiation
- **Extra help:** a finished game with a local high score (no leaderboard or AI feature) can still reach level 3 on most rows.
- **Extra challenge:** add a "daily challenge" level that's the same for everyone each day, with its own leaderboard.
- **1-on-1 tip:** real players make this a day the student will remember. Even 2 guests competing on the leaderboard is a big moment.
