## Lesson objectives
By the end, the student can…
- Make original, consistent game art with a **character sheet** and **style line** (from Module 1).
- Add sound effects and music they have the right to use, and keep a **credits list**.
- Add game juice that fits the mood of their game.

## Words for today
- **Sprite** (again) — a picture of one thing in the game.
- **Sprite sheet** — one picture with many poses of a character, for moving pictures.
- **Art style** — the look of the whole game (pixel art, soft pastel, bold cartoon).
- **License** — the rules for using someone else's sound or picture.
- **Credits** — a list of who made what in your game.

## 1. Warm-up — Same game, new mood (10 min · 1-on-1: 5 min)
Look at your gray-box game. Imagine it in 3 styles: cozy, spooky, silly. How would each change the music, colors and sounds?

```solo
You both describe the game in a style, then guess which one the other picked. Which style fits the student's game plan best?
```

## 2. Mini-lesson — Look, sound, feel (15 min · 1-on-1: 10 min)
**Art** (**Visual 1**), using Module 1 skills:
- Write a **character sheet** for your main character and a **style line** for the whole game.
- Make sprites in Gemini with a **plain or transparent background**, the **same style line every time**, and a simple, clear shape (games are small on phones!).
- Backgrounds: wide, calm, not too busy, so the player stands out.

**Sound:**
- Every action gets a short sound: jump, collect, hit, win, lose.
- Music sets the mood. Keep it quieter than the effects, and loop it smoothly.
- Use sounds you made, or free sounds whose **license** lets you use them. Write them in your **credits**.

**Game feel:** match the juice to the mood: soft sparkles for cozy, sharp shakes for action. Always add **mute** and **effects off** options.

## 3. Hands-on build — Make it yours (35 min · 1-on-1: 25 min)
1. Write your character sheet + style line. Make the main sprite in Gemini. Pick the best one.
2. Make 2 or 3 more sprites (things to collect or avoid) and one background, with the same style line.
3. Find or record 5 sounds (jump, collect, hit, win, lose) and one music loop. Add each to your credits list.
4. Ask Claude Code:
```
Add art and sound to my game. Replace the shapes with these images: [list files and what each is]. Add these sounds for these actions: [list]. Add background music that loops, quieter than the effects. Add mute and "effects off" buttons. Add 2 juice effects that fit a [cozy/action/silly] mood. Make a Credits screen with this list: [paste]. Keep all game rules the same.
```
5. Play after each change. Deploy.

**Checkpoint 1 (+20 XP):** original art in the game, with a consistent style.
**Checkpoint 2 (+20 XP):** 5 sounds + music + mute, and a Credits screen.

```group
Pairs check each other's credits: is every sound and picture listed and allowed?
```

```solo
Record one sound effect together with a phone (a pop, a clap, a funny "boing"). Homemade sounds are 100% yours and often the most fun.
```

> 🛡️ **License moment:** "free" doesn't always mean "free to use anywhere". Read the license. If you can't tell, don't use it: make your own instead.

## 4. Game / challenge — Sound Only (15 min · 1-on-1: 10 min)
A player plays your game with their eyes closed for 10 seconds while you play it. Can they tell what's happening from sound alone? (Jump? Collect? Hit?)

```group
Most actions guessed right: +15 XP. Everyone: +5.
```

```solo
**Student vs. Bolt.** Take turns playing while the other listens with eyes closed and calls out each action. +15 XP for playing, +5 for the most right guesses.
```

## 5. Share-out — Before and after (10 min · 1-on-1: 5 min)
Show 10 seconds of the gray-box game and 10 seconds of the new version.

```solo
Screen-record the before and after for the portfolio. It's a great "wow" clip.
```

## 6. Exit ticket (5 min)
1. How did you keep your sprites looking like the same game?
2. What's a license, and why does it matter?
3. Which sound makes your game feel best?

```solo
Ask out loud. Check the Credits screen is complete.
```

```prompt
A game art style sheet, 16:9, titled "MY GAME'S STYLE": an original cute round gardener character in 3 poses (standing, jumping, waving), 3 matching items (seed, watering can, bug), and a calm garden background strip, all in the same soft pastel pixel-art style. Color swatches at the bottom. No real game characters.
```

**Visual 2 — Every action has a sound.**
```prompt
A playful chart, 16:9, titled "EVERY ACTION HAS A SOUND". Five icons with comic sound words: jump "boing!", collect "ding!", hit "bonk!", win "ta-da!", lose "womp womp". A small speaker with a mute button in the corner. Bright flat style, readable.
```

## Differentiation
- **Extra help:** one sprite (the player) + 3 sounds + mute. Keep shapes for everything else.
- **Extra challenge:** make a sprite sheet so your character has a walking motion, and ask Claude Code to play it.
- **1-on-1 tip:** reuse the student's mascot from Module 1 if it fits. It ties their whole year together.
