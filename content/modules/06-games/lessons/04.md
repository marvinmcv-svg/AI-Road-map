## Lesson objectives
By the end, the student can…
- Add a **twist** that changes the core loop, not just the look.
- Add **game juice**: sounds, shakes, sparkles and other feedback that makes actions feel good.
- Run a short **playtest** and use the notes to improve.

## Words for today
- **Twist** — a new rule or mechanic that makes a familiar game feel new.
- **Game juice** — extra feedback that makes actions feel great: a pop, a sparkle, a tiny shake.
- **Playtest** — watching someone play your game to see what works.
- **Particle** — a tiny dot or spark that flies out (like confetti when you score).
- **Screen shake** — the screen wiggles a tiny bit on a big hit.

## 1. Warm-up — Dry vs. juicy (10 min · 1-on-1: 5 min)
```teacher
Game juice is the shake, sound and sparkle that make a game feel alive. Today you add your twist and your juice. Feel the difference.
```

Play your demo remix twice: once with juice turned off, once with juice on. Which feels better? Why?

```solo
Play both versions and describe the difference in 3 words each.
```

## 2. Mini-lesson — Twists and juice (10 min · 1-on-1: 7 min)
**Twist ideas** (**Visual 1**):
- 🔄 Flip a rule: the ball gets SLOWER as you score, but the paddle shrinks.
- ➕ Add a mechanic: you can dash, you can stick to walls, the maze changes every 30 seconds.
- 👥 New way to play: 2 players on one screen, or you're the chaser instead of the chased.
- ⏱️ New goal: survive 60 seconds, collect exactly 7, never touch the same color twice.

**Juice ideas** (**Visual 2**): a sound for every hit, particles when you score, a tiny screen shake on a crash, the score number "pops", a short slow-motion on game over.

Juice should be **quick and small**. Too much is tiring. And add a **mute** button!

## 3. Hands-on build — Twist + juice (35 min · 1-on-1: 25 min)
1. Ask Claude to add your twist. Play it 3 times. Does it change how you play?
2. Ask for 3 juice effects, one at a time. Play after each.
3. Add a mute button and a pause button.
4. Optional: make 1 or 2 original sprites in Gemini (same style) and ask Claude to use them.
5. Publish the artifact.

**Checkpoint 1 (+20 XP):** twist added and changes the core loop.
**Checkpoint 2 (+20 XP):** 3 juice effects + mute and pause, published.

```group
Pairs play each other's twist and say if it changes how they play.
```

```solo
Play it with the student and say "ooh" when the juice lands. When it's too much, say so. They tune it down.
```

> 🛡️ **Comfort moment:** flashing lights and big shakes can make some people feel sick. Keep flashes gentle, and let players turn effects off.

## 4. Game / challenge — The Remix Arcade (20 min · 1-on-1: 13 min)
Set up the games on laptops and phones. Players visit each game for 3 minutes and fill in a Playtest Card. Makers just watch and take notes. No explaining!

```group
Awards: "Best Twist", "Juiciest Game", "Most 'One More Try'" (+15 house XP each). Everyone who plays 3 games: +5.
```

```solo
**Arcade night.** You, plus a guest if possible, play the remix while the student watches silently and takes notes. Then they play your demo remix and fill in a Playtest Card for you. +15 XP for running the arcade, +5 for great notes.
```

## 5. Share-out — What I learned (10 min · 1-on-1: 5 min)
Read your playtest notes. What surprised you? What will you do in your OWN game?

```solo
Look at the notes together and pick the one lesson the student wants to carry into their own game.
```

## 6. Exit ticket (5 min)
1. What's the difference between a twist and a new look?
2. Name 3 kinds of game juice.
3. What did the playtest teach you?

```solo
Ask out loud. The remix is done. Next, we design your own game from scratch!
```

## Differentiation
- **Extra help:** one twist + one juice effect is enough. Use ready-made twist cards.
- **Extra challenge:** add a second twist that unlocks after 30 seconds, so the game changes as you play.
- **1-on-1 tip:** silent watching is hard but powerful. Practice it here: it's the core skill for the rest of the module.
