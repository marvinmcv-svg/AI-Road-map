## 🔥 Warm-up: Something's off
Play an unfair game. What's wrong? Which number would you change?

## 💡 Big idea: balance like a designer
1. 📋 **Settings table** — every tuning number in one place
2. 📊 **Collect numbers** — level reached, score, time, what made you lose
3. 🔎 **Spot the problem** — a level nobody beats? a power-up that always wins?
4. 🎚️ **Change ONE number** — then measure again

**Good luck:** where things appear. **Bad luck:** losing with nothing you could do.

```try
Here are my playtest stats and settings: [paste]. What do you notice? Which ONE setting would you change first, and why?
```
