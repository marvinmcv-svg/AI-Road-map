## 🔥 Warm-up: Dry vs. juicy
Play the same game with and without effects. Which feels better? Why?

## 💡 Big idea
**Twist ideas:** 🔄 flip a rule · ➕ add a mechanic · 👥 a new way to play · ⏱️ a new goal

**Game juice:** a sound for every hit · sparkles when you score · a tiny screen shake · the score "pops"

Keep juice quick and small. Always add **mute** and **pause**!

```try
Add this twist to my game: every 10 points, the bubble splits into two. Then add 3 small juice effects: a pop sound on each bounce, sparkles when I score, and a tiny screen shake when I lose a life. Add mute and pause buttons.
```

## 🛠️ Your build
- [ ] Add your twist
- [ ] Play it 3 times: does it change how you play?
- [ ] Add 3 juice effects, one at a time
- [ ] Add mute and pause buttons
- [ ] Publish your remix

## ⚔️ Challenge: The Remix Arcade
Players try your game for 3 minutes. You watch silently and take notes. No explaining!

```group
Visit 3 games and fill in a Playtest Card for each.
```

```solo
Your teacher (and a guest) play your remix while you take notes. Then you playtest your teacher's remix!
```

## 🎟️ Exit ticket
1. What's the difference between a twist and a new look?
2. Name 3 kinds of game juice.
3. What did the playtest teach you?

## 🛡️ Stay safe
Big flashes and shakes can make people feel sick. Keep them gentle, and let players turn them off.
