## 🔥 Warm-up: Play and pause
Play 2 short games. What were you trying to do? What kept you playing?
📣 **Final quest:** design and ship your own game!

## 💡 Big idea: every game has…
- 🎯 **Goal** — what you're trying to do
- 📜 **Rules** — what you can and can't do
- 🧗 **Challenge** — what makes it hard
- ✨ **Feedback** — how you know how you're doing (points, sounds, flashes)

**Core loop:** the small set of actions you repeat. *Run → jump → collect → repeat.*
**Fun = challenge that's just right.** Too easy = boring. Too hard = stressful.

```try
Here's my paper game: [your rules]. What's the core loop? Suggest 3 small rule changes that could make it more fun.
```
