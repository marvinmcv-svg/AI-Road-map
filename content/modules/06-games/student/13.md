## 🔥 Warm-up: Word Lightning Round
Your teacher shows a word. Say what it means and give an example. Fast!

## 💡 What "Pro" looks like
Look at the rubric with your teacher, one row at a time.

## 🛠️ Your build: Launch checklist
- [ ] Original name, characters, art and sounds, with credits
- [ ] The core loop is fun in the first 30 seconds
- [ ] Start, pause, game over and play again all work
- [ ] 3+ levels that teach → practice → test
- [ ] Balanced with real playtest numbers
- [ ] High score and a fair leaderboard
- [ ] AI feature (if any) is safe, with limits and fallbacks
- [ ] Fair & Fun checklist passes
- [ ] Works on phone and computer
- [ ] Score yourself with the rubric and fix your lowest row

## ⚔️ Challenge: Game Launch Arcade
Your 3-minute trailer pitch:
1. Show your best 20 seconds of play
2. "It's a ___ where you ___ to ___."
3. "Playtesters struggled with ___, so I changed ___."
4. "Claude Code built ___. I designed ___."
5. Share the link!

```group
Every guest plays and leaves a ⭐ strength, a ❓ question and their high score.
```

```solo
Count down "3, 2, 1, launch!" and share your link with your teacher and guests. Watch your leaderboard fill up!
```

## 🎟️ Exit ticket
1. What part of your game are you most proud of? Why?
2. What did playtesting teach you?
3. What would you add with one more week?

## 🛡️ Stay safe
Keep checking your leaderboard for rude names after launch, and remove them.
