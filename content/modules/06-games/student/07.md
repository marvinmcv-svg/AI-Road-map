## 🔥 Warm-up: Same game, new mood
Imagine your game as cozy, spooky or silly. How would the colors and sounds change?

## 💡 Big idea
🎨 **Art:** a character sheet + a style line (from Module 1). Same style every time. Simple shapes that read well on a phone.

🔊 **Sound:** every action gets a sound (jump, collect, hit, win, lose). Music quieter than the effects.

📜 **Credits:** only use sounds and pictures you made, or ones whose **license** lets you use them. List them all!

```try
Simple pixel-art sprite of a round, cheerful gardener with a big straw hat, soft pastel colors, facing right, plain white background, no words, square 1:1.
```

## 🛠️ Your build
- [ ] Write your character sheet and style line
- [ ] Make your main sprite and 2 or 3 more in Gemini, same style
- [ ] Make one background
- [ ] Find or record 5 sounds and one music loop
- [ ] Write a credits list
- [ ] Ask Claude Code to add the art, sounds, mute and a Credits screen

## ⚔️ Challenge: Sound Only
Someone listens with eyes closed while you play. Can they tell what's happening?

```group
Most actions guessed right wins.
```

```solo
**You vs. your teacher.** Take turns playing and listening!
```

## 🎟️ Exit ticket
1. How did you keep your sprites looking like the same game?
2. What's a license, and why does it matter?
3. Which sound makes your game feel best?

## 🛡️ Stay safe
"Free" doesn't always mean "free to use". If you can't tell, make your own sound instead.
