## 🔥 Warm-up: Play the demo
Play your teacher's game on a computer and a phone. Name its goal, rules, challenge and feedback.

## 💡 Big idea: the game loop
About 60 times every second, a game does 3 things:
1. ⌨️ **Input** — which keys or taps?
2. ⚙️ **Update** — move things, check **collisions**, add points
3. 🎨 **Draw** — show everything in its new place

**Numbers make the feel:** speed, how often things appear, sizes. Change one at a time!

```try
Make a small, original browser game as an artifact: a cat catching falling fish. Controls: arrow keys AND touch. Lose when you miss 3 fish. Show the score and a "Play again" button. Keep it simple, bright and cute. Put the important numbers at the top so I can change them easily.
```
