## 🔥 Warm-up: Idea Sparks
Draw a verb and a theme card. Sketch a game that uses both in 3 minutes!

## 💡 Big idea: the One-Page Game Plan
- 💡 **Pitch** — "It's a ___ where you ___ to ___."
- 🙋 **Player** — who is it for?
- 🔁 **Core loop** — what do you repeat?
- ⚙️ **Mechanics** — what can the player do?
- 🏆 **Win / lose** — how do you win? how do you lose?
- 🎮 **Controls** — keys, taps, swipes
- 🎨 **Look & sound** — the mood
- ✂️ **Scope** — what you're NOT making (keep it small!)

```try
Here's my game plan: [paste]. What's unclear? What could be boring after 2 minutes? What's too big for 6 lessons? Suggest how to make the scope smaller.
```

## 🛠️ Your build
- [ ] Fill in your One-Page Game Plan
- [ ] Ask Claude to poke holes in it
- [ ] Cut anything too big (put it on a Later list)
- [ ] Test the core loop on paper or as a gray-box artifact
- [ ] Is it fun for 30 seconds? Change one thing and try again

## ⚔️ Challenge: Pitch and Poke
Pitch your game in 30 seconds. Listeners ask "What happens if…?" questions.

```group
The class votes for the best pitch.
```

```solo
**You vs. your teacher.** Pitch and poke each other's games!
```

## 🎟️ Exit ticket
1. What's your elevator pitch?
2. What's your core loop?
3. What did you cut to keep the scope small?

## 🛡️ Stay safe
Your game should be your own idea, with your own name, characters and world.

## 🚀 Bonus quest (+25 XP)
