## 💡 Big idea
🏅 **Local high score** — saved on this device
🌍 **Online leaderboard** — a Supabase table: anyone can read the top 10, players can add a score, nobody can change others' scores (RLS!)

**Fair Leaderboard checklist:**
- 🙋 Nicknames only
- 🧹 Block rude words, max 12 letters
- 🔢 Scores can't be higher than possible
- 🐢 One score per finished game
- 🗑️ You can remove bad entries
