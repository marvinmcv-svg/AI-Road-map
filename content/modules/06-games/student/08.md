## 💡 Big idea: the flow zone
Too hard = stress. Too easy = boring. Just right = **flow**!

**Teach → practice → test:**
1. 🌱 **Teach** one new idea in a safe place
2. 🔁 **Practice** it a few times, a little harder
3. 🏆 **Test** it with something you learned before

Make ONE thing harder at a time. Add an easy moment after a hard part.

```try
Add 3 levels to my game. Level 1 teaches planting safely with a short hint on screen. Level 2 practices it, a little faster. Level 3 tests planting while shooing bugs. Put all level settings in one easy-to-edit list. Show "Level 2!" between levels and keep the score.
```
