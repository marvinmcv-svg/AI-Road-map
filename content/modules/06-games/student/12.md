## 🔥 Warm-up: Fair or sneaky?
Look at game designs: a "pay now or wait 4 hours" timer, a streak you lose if you miss a day, a one-time price. Which feel fair?

## 💡 Big idea: two checklists
**Bug triage:** 🔴 game-breaking → fix now · 🟠 annoying → fix next · 🟢 tiny → if there's time

**Fair & Fun:**
- ♿ **Access** — keyboard AND touch, pause, easy mode, effects off, color plus icons
- 🤝 **Fair** — clear rules, no losing from pure luck
- 💛 **Kind** — no guilt timers, no pay-to-win, no loot boxes, credits for everything
