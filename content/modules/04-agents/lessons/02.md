## Lesson objectives
By the end, the student can…
- Explain what a **Claude Project** is and why giving it **instructions** and **facts** makes it better.
- Write helper instructions with 6 parts.
- Give the Project a Store Facts file and test it with 10 questions.

## Words for today
- **Claude Project** — your own helper space in Claude, with saved instructions and files it uses in every chat.
- **Instructions (system prompt)** — the rules the helper follows every time, before anyone types.
- **Project knowledge** — files the helper reads to get facts right (like your Store Facts doc).
- **Grounding** — making the AI answer from YOUR facts, not from guessing.
- **Test question** — a question you ask on purpose to check the helper.

## 1. Warm-up — Plain vs. helper (10 min · 1-on-1: 5 min)
```teacher
Today you build your own AI helper for your store. You give it rules and facts, and it answers for you. First, watch what happens without facts.
```

Ask Claude in a plain chat: "How long does shipping take for Sip Squad bottles?" It can't know! It may guess (a hallucination). Then ask the same thing in your demo Project. It answers from the facts file.

```solo
The student asks both, then tries to trick the demo Project with a question it doesn't have facts for. What does it do?
```

## 2. Mini-lesson — The Helper Instructions (12 min · 1-on-1: 8 min)
Show **Visual 1**. Good instructions have 6 parts:
1. **Role:** "You are the friendly helper for Sip Squad, an online water bottle store."
2. **Job:** "You answer customer questions about products, orders, shipping and returns."
3. **Facts:** "Use ONLY the Store Facts file. If the answer isn't there, say you'll ask a person."
4. **Tone:** "Short, warm, clear. Use the brand's personality words."
5. **Rules:** "Never promise refunds. Never ask for card numbers or passwords. Never make up a price or a date."
6. **When to ask a human:** "If the customer is upset, wants a refund, or asks something not in the facts, say: 'Let me check with the team and get back to you.'"

**Store Facts file** (**Visual 2**): products (names, sizes, colors, prices), shipping (time, cost), returns, contact, a short FAQ. Only true facts!

## 3. Hands-on build — Make your Claude Project (43 min · 1-on-1: 30 min)
1. Write your **Store Facts** doc from your Module 3 site (10 min).
2. In Claude, open **Projects → New project**. Name it ("Sip Squad Helper").
3. Write the 6-part instructions in the Project's instructions box (or ask Claude to draft them from the template, then fix them).
4. Add the Store Facts doc to the Project's knowledge.
5. **Test it** with the 10 test questions. For each answer, mark: ✅ right · ⚠️ partly · ❌ wrong or made up.
6. Fix the instructions or the facts file, then test the ❌ ones again.

**Checkpoint 1 (+20 XP):** Project made with all 6 instruction parts and the facts file.
**Checkpoint 2 (+20 XP):** 10 test questions scored, and 2 or more fixes made.

```group
Pairs swap Projects (or copy each other's instructions into a new Project) and ask each other's questions.
```

```solo
You play the customer for the 10 test questions. Act out a few moods (in a hurry, confused, upset). The student scores each answer and decides what to fix.
```

> 🛡️ **Facts moment:** put only store facts in the knowledge file. No real customer names, emails or orders.

## 4. Game / challenge — Stump the Helper (10 min · 1-on-1: 7 min)
Write 3 tricky questions to trip up a helper: something not in the facts, a refund demand, a question about a different company. Does the helper stay honest and ask a human when it should?

```group
Houses try to stump each other's helpers. Each helper that handles all 3 well: +15 XP. Everyone: +5.
```

```solo
**Student vs. Bolt.** You write 3 tricky questions for their helper; they write 3 for your demo helper. Whose helper handles them better? +15 XP for playing, +5 for the win.
```

## 5. Share-out — Meet my helper (10 min · 1-on-1: 5 min)
Show your helper answering one easy and one tricky question.

```solo
The student shows you their best tricky-question save. Ask: "What instruction made that work?"
```

## 6. Exit ticket (5 min)
1. What's a Claude Project?
2. Name 4 of the 6 instruction parts.
3. What should the helper do when the answer isn't in the facts?

```solo
Ask out loud. Keep the test questions. You'll reuse them next lesson.
```

## Differentiation
- **Extra help:** start from a filled-in instructions template and just change the store details. 5 test questions is enough.
- **Extra challenge:** make a second Project for a different job (social media post writer in your brand voice) and test it on 5 posts.
- **1-on-1 tip:** show the student your demo Project's instructions so they can see how they are written.
