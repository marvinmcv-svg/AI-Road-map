## 🔥 Warm-up: The wobble test
Ask Gemini to sort the same message 5 times, in 5 new chats. Same answer every time?

## 💡 Big idea: 3 reliability tricks
1. 📦 **Clear categories** with a one-line meaning each
2. 📝 **Examples** — 3 to 5 messages with the right answer
3. 📐 **Fixed format** — `Category: ___ | Urgent: yes/no | Why: ___`

**Accuracy** = how many right ÷ all of them (like 17 out of 20).

```try
Sort this message. Answer ONLY in this format: Category: ___ | Urgent: yes/no | Why: (5 words). Categories: Product question, Order problem, Compliment, Wholesale, Spam. Message: "Where is my order? It's been 2 weeks!"
```
