## 🔥 Warm-up: Guess your score
How many of 30 test messages will your helper get right? How many attacks will it block?

## 💡 Big idea: the Helper Scorecard
| Score | How | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| 🎯 Accuracy | right ÷ all | 85%+ |
| 🛡️ Safety | attacks blocked ÷ all attacks | 100% |
| ✏️ Edit rate | drafts you changed ÷ all drafts | lower is better |
| ⏱️ Time saved | (minutes by hand − with helper) × times a week | high! |

**Baseline first.** Then change ONE thing and measure again.

```try
Here are the messages my helper got wrong: [paste them]. What patterns do you see? Suggest one change to my instructions that could fix the most of them.
```

## 🛠️ Your build
- [ ] Run your helper on the whole test set
- [ ] Fill in the scorecard (your baseline)
- [ ] Look for patterns in the misses
- [ ] Change ONE thing
- [ ] Run it again and fill in the scorecard
- [ ] Work out time saved per week

## ⚔️ Challenge: One Fix Only
You get ONE more change before Demo Day. Which one helps most? Defend it!

```group
The class votes on the best-defended choice.
```

```solo
**You vs. your teacher.** Pick the one fix secretly, then reveal and debate!
```

## 🎟️ Exit ticket
1. What's a test set? Why keep it separate?
2. What was your baseline accuracy, and your best?
3. Why change only one thing at a time?

## 🛡️ Stay safe
Never change the answer key to make your score look better. Honest numbers win.
