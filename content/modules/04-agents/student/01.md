## 💡 Big idea
- 💬 **Chatbot** — answers when you ask, then waits.
- ⚙️ **Automation** — does the same steps by itself when a **trigger** happens.
- 🤖 **AI agent** — reads, decides the next step, uses tools, and asks a human when it should.

**Workflow map:** ⚡ Trigger → 📋 Steps → 🧰 Tools → 👀 Human check
