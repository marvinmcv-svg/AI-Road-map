## 🔥 Warm-up: Plain vs. helper
Ask plain Gemini how long your store's shipping takes. It can't know! Now ask your teacher's demo Gem.

## 💡 Big idea: Helper Instructions
1. 🏷️ **Role** — who the helper is
2. 💼 **Job** — what it does
3. 📚 **Facts** — use ONLY the Store Facts file
4. 😊 **Tone** — how it sounds
5. 🛡️ **Rules** — what it must never do
6. ✋ **When to ask a human** — upset customers, refunds, unknown answers

```try
You are the friendly helper for Sip Squad, an online water bottle store. You answer customer questions about products, orders, shipping and returns. Use ONLY the Store Facts file. If the answer isn't there, say "Let me check with the team and get back to you." Short, warm and clear. Never promise refunds. Never ask for card numbers or passwords. Never make up a price or a date.
```

## 🛠️ Your build
- [ ] Write a Store Facts doc (products, prices, shipping, returns, contact, FAQ)
- [ ] Open Gems → New Gem and name it
- [ ] Write all 6 instruction parts
- [ ] Add the Store Facts doc as a file
- [ ] Test it with 10 questions: ✅ right · ⚠️ partly · ❌ wrong
- [ ] Fix and test the ❌ ones again

## ⚔️ Challenge: Stump the Gem
Write 3 tricky questions. Does the helper stay honest and ask a human when it should?

```group
Try to stump other houses' Gems.
```

```solo
**You vs. your teacher.** Swap 3 tricky questions. Whose Gem handles them better?
```

## 🎟️ Exit ticket
1. What's a Gem?
2. Name 4 of the 6 instruction parts.
3. What should the helper do when the answer isn't in the facts?

## 🛡️ Stay safe
Only store facts go in the file. No real customer names, emails or orders.

## 🚀 Bonus quest (+25 XP)
Build a second Gem that writes social posts in your brand voice. Test it on 5 posts.
