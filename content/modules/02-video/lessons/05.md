## Lesson objectives
By the end, the student can…
- Name the 3 kinds of video sound: **voice**, **music** and **sound effects**.
- Write a voiceover script that fits the time (about 2 to 3 words per second).
- Record a voiceover (own voice or AI voice) and pick music that matches the mood.
- Explain why copying a real person's voice without a "yes" is not OK.

## Words for today
- **Voiceover** — a voice that talks over the video. You hear it, but you don't see who's talking.
- **Script** — the exact words someone will say.
- **Sound effect (SFX)** — a short sound, like a splash, a click or a whoosh.
- **Background music** — music under the video that sets the mood.
- **Voice copy (voice cloning)** — AI that makes a voice sound like a real person.

## 1. Warm-up — Same clip, new feeling (10 min · 1-on-1: 5 min)
Play one ad 3 times: with its sound, with no sound, then with spooky or silly music. Ask: "How did the feeling change? Which one would make you buy it?"

```group
Everyone writes one word for the feeling each time. Compare words.
```

```solo
After the 3 plays, let the student pick a 4th music style for you to try. Talk about how much sound changed everything.
```

## 2. Mini-lesson — The 3 kinds of sound (15 min · 1-on-1: 10 min)
Show **Visual 1**:
1. 🗣️ **Voice** — tells the story. Short, clear sentences. Smile when you talk. People can hear it!
2. 🎵 **Music** — sets the mood. Fast = fun and exciting. Slow = calm or serious. Keep it quieter than the voice.
3. 💥 **Sound effects** — make actions feel real: a splash when water pours, a "ding" when the logo shows.

**The timing rule:** people say about **2 to 3 words every second**. So a 30-second ad has room for about **60 to 75 words**. Leave quiet gaps, too.

**Ways to get sound:**
- Veo clips from Gemini already come with sound. Ask for it in the Sound part of the prompt.
- Record your own voice in **Google Vids**.
- Or pick an **AI voice** in Google Vids and type the script.

```teacher
Here's a secret: in most ads, you remember the sound more than the pictures. A great voice and the right music can make a so-so video feel amazing.
```

**Voice copy rule:** AI can now copy a real person's voice from a short recording. That can be used to trick people, like a fake phone call from "Grandma". **Our rule: we only use our own voice or a preset AI voice. Never a copy of a real person's voice.**

## 3. Hands-on build — Write it, voice it (35 min · 1-on-1: 25 min)
1. **Script (10 min):** ask Gemini: "Write a 30-second voiceover for my ad. Here's my storyboard: [paste the 4 beats]. Use about 65 words, short sentences, fun and friendly, for [buyers]. Mark which storyboard box each line goes with." Then fix it so it sounds like YOU.
2. **Read it out loud with a timer (5 min).** Too long? Cut words, not speed.
3. **Record it (10 min):** in Google Vids, record your own voice. Or try an AI voice and compare. Pick the one that fits the brand.
4. **Music and sound effects (10 min):** pick background music that matches the brand's personality words. Note 2 sound effects you want (and in which box).

**Checkpoint 1 (+20 XP):** script written and timed (fits in 30 seconds).
**Checkpoint 2 (+20 XP):** voiceover recorded + music picked + 2 sound effects planned.

```group
Take turns in the quiet corner to record. Others work on music while they wait.
```

```solo
Be the sound engineer: you hold the timer and give a thumbs up when the take sounds good. Let them do 3 takes and pick the best. If they're shy about their own voice, an AI voice is fine. Just ask them to try their own voice once.
```

## 4. Game / challenge — Sound Match (15 min · 1-on-1: 10 min)
Play 4 silent clips. For each one, pick the best music and sound effect from a list (or make one with your mouth!). Explain why it fits.

```group
Houses perform their sound effects live while the clip plays. The class votes for the best match. +15 XP to the winner. Everyone: +5.
```

```solo
**Live foley!** Play a silent clip. The student makes the sound effects live with their voice and objects on the desk (tap a cup for footsteps, blow for wind). Then you try one. Laugh a lot. +15 XP for playing, +5 for the best sound of the day.
```

## 5. Share-out — Listening party (10 min · 1-on-1: 5 min)
Play the voiceover with eyes closed. Ask: "Can you picture the ad just from the sound? Which word stuck in your head?"

```solo
Play the voiceover once with eyes closed, then once with the storyboard. The student picks the one line they're proudest of.
```

## 6. Exit ticket (5 min)
1. Name the 3 kinds of video sound.
2. About how many words fit in a 30-second ad?
3. Why do we never copy a real person's voice?

```solo
Ask out loud. For #3, ask them to think of one way a fake voice could hurt someone.
```

## Differentiation
- **Extra help:** give a script starter with blanks: "Ever ___? Meet ___. It ___. Get yours at ___!" A 15-second ad is OK.
- **Extra challenge:** write 2 scripts with different moods (funny vs. calm). Record both. Ask 3 people which works better.
- **1-on-1 tip:** record in short pieces (one storyboard box at a time). It's much less scary than one long take.
