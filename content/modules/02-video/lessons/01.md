## Lesson objectives
By the end, the student can…
- Say that a video is many **frames** shown fast, and why AI video is harder than one picture.
- Write a video prompt with all 6 parts of the **Video Prompt Formula**.
- Make an 8-second clip in Gemini and say which words changed it.

## Words for today
- **Frame** — one still picture in a video. Most videos show about 24 frames every second.
- **Clip** — one short piece of video. Gemini makes clips about 8 seconds long.
- **Motion** — how things move in the clip.
- **Same from frame to frame** — the AI has to keep faces, colors and objects matching the whole time.
- **Shape (aspect ratio)** — wide 16:9 for screens, tall 9:16 for phones.

## 1. Warm-up — Flip book (10 min · 1-on-1: 5 min)
Draw a stick figure on 6 sticky notes. Move one arm a little on each note. Flip them fast. It moves!
Then ask: "A video is just pictures flipping fast. What would be hard for an AI about that?"

```teacher
Welcome to Module 2! Your company loved its new look. Now it wants a launch video, a short ad people actually watch. To make it, you need to know how AI turns words into moving pictures.
```

```group
Pairs make one flip book together. Collect answers to "what's hard for the AI?" on the board.
```

```solo
You each make a flip book and race. Whose moves more smoothly? Then ask the student: "What made yours look jumpy?" That's the same problem AI has.
```

## 2. Mini-lesson — How AI makes a clip (15 min · 1-on-1: 10 min)
1. Show **Visual 1**: a strip of frames. A video is about 24 pictures every second. An 8-second clip is about 200 pictures!
2. Remember the picture lesson: AI starts with noise and cleans it into a picture. Video AI does that for **all the frames at once**. It also has to keep them matching. The dog can't turn into a cat halfway through.
3. That's why AI video sometimes has glitches: things melt, hands change, words wiggle. Shorter, simpler clips work best.
4. Gemini's video maker (called **Veo**) makes about 8 seconds at a time, **with sound**. Later we'll glue clips together into a longer video.
5. Show the **Video Prompt Formula** (**Visual 2**):

| Part | Ask yourself | Word ideas |
|---|---|---|
| 🎬 **Subject & action** | Who or what? Doing what? | a robot waters a tiny plant |
| 📍 **Setting** | Where? When? | on a sunny windowsill in the morning |
| 🎨 **Style** | What look? | 3D cartoon, real-life video, clay, anime-style |
| 🎥 **Camera** | How close? Does it move? | close-up, slowly zooms in |
| ☀️ **Light & mood** | How should it feel? | warm and calm, bright and fun |
| 🔊 **Sound** | What do we hear? | birds chirping, soft piano, a happy beep |

Pick the **shape** too: wide 16:9 or tall 9:16.

Example: *"A small round robot waters a tiny plant (subject & action) on a sunny windowsill in the morning (setting), 3D cartoon (style), close-up, the camera slowly zooms in (camera), warm and calm (light & mood), soft piano music and a happy beep (sound), 16:9."*

```solo
Play your two example clips (short prompt vs. all 6 parts). Ask the student which parts of the formula they can "hear" and "see" in the better one.
```

## 3. Hands-on build — First clips (35 min · 1-on-1: 25 min)
Make every clip in **Gemini**.
1. **Copy and run** the robot prompt. Save the clip.
2. **Change ONE part at a time.** Guess before you press Enter:
   - Change the **Style** → what changed?
   - Change the **Camera** → what changed?
   - Change the **Sound** → what changed?
3. **Your own clip:** write a 6-part prompt starring your company's product or mascot from Module 1.
4. Write it all in the log:

| # | Part I changed | My guess | What really happened | Any glitches? |
|---|---|---|---|---|

**Checkpoint 1 (+20 XP):** 3 one-change tests written down with guesses.
**Checkpoint 2 (+20 XP):** the student's own clip saved in their folder, named with *#MadeWithAI*.

Clips take a minute or two to make. While one is cooking, write the next prompt.

```group
Everyone works alone. Walk around and ask: "What do you think will change?"
```

```solo
Sit side by side. Let the student type every prompt. While a clip is loading, ask "What's your guess?" Watch each clip twice: once for the picture, once with eyes closed, just for the sound.
```

> 🛡️ **Daily limit moment:** video takes a lot of computer power, so Gemini only gives a few clips a day. Plan your prompt BEFORE you press Enter. Don't waste clips on "let's just see".

## 4. Game / challenge — Name That Prompt (15 min · 1-on-1: 10 min)
Play 3 mystery clips. For each one, write the 6 formula parts that probably made it. One point for each part you get right.

```group
Houses play. Most points: +15 XP. Everyone who finishes: +5.
```

```solo
**Student vs. Bolt.** You both write your guesses on paper, then compare with the real prompt. Next, the student makes a mystery clip and YOU guess its prompt. +15 XP for playing, +5 for the win.
```

## 5. Share-out — Clip screening (10 min · 1-on-1: 5 min)
Watch the student's own clip. Feedback lines: *"Your best formula part is… / One part to make clearer is…"*

```group
Play 4 or 5 clips on the big screen. Classmates give feedback cards.
```

```solo
Play the clip. The student tells you their favorite part. You give one "best part" and one "make it clearer". Write the better prompt together for next time.
```

## 6. Exit ticket (5 min)
1. What is a frame?
2. Why is making a video harder for AI than making one picture?
3. Name the 6 parts of the Video Prompt Formula.

```solo
Ask out loud. If #2 is fuzzy, flip the flip book again and ask what would happen if the stick figure changed shape halfway.
```

## Visuals to show
**Visual 1 — Frames strip.**
```prompt
An educational illustration, 16:9, titled "A VIDEO = LOTS OF PICTURES". A long film strip runs across the image with 8 frames showing a cartoon robot raising its arm a little more in each frame. Above it the label "24 frames every second". Below, a small stopwatch and the text "8 seconds = about 200 pictures!". Bright flat colors, bold rounded labels, white background.
```

**Visual 2 — Video Prompt Formula poster.**
```prompt
A classroom poster titled "THE VIDEO PROMPT FORMULA", portrait 3:4. Six colorful stacked blocks like a burger, each with an icon and label: SUBJECT & ACTION (clapperboard), SETTING (map pin), STYLE (paint palette), CAMERA (movie camera with arrows), LIGHT & MOOD (sun), SOUND (speaker). Each block has a tiny example in smaller text. Playful flat design, bright colors, very readable.
```

**Visual 3 — Common AI video glitches.**
```prompt
A friendly infographic, 16:9, titled "AI VIDEO GLITCHES TO SPOT". Four panels: "MELTING" a cartoon cup losing its shape, "CHANGING" a dog that turns into a cat between two frames, "WIGGLY WORDS" a sign with letters moving around, "EXTRA PARTS" a cartoon hand with six fingers. Each panel has a small red circle on the glitch. Flat cartoon style, bright colors, bold labels, white background.
```

## Differentiation
- **Extra help:** give the formula card with word lists for each part. Change only Style and Sound.
- **Extra challenge:** make the same prompt in 16:9 and 9:16. What did Gemini move around to fit the new shape?
- **1-on-1 tip:** clips take a minute to make, so use the waiting time to talk. Ask about the student's favorite ads and why they work.
