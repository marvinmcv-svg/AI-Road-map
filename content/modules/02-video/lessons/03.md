## Lesson objectives
By the end, the student can…
- Name 6 camera moves and the feeling each one gives.
- Put the right camera move into a video prompt.
- Make 2 or more storyboard shots as Gemini clips.

## Words for today
- **Zoom in / zoom out** — the camera gets closer, or moves away.
- **Pan** — the camera turns left or right, like turning your head.
- **Follow (tracking shot)** — the camera moves along with someone.
- **Circle around (orbit)** — the camera goes around the subject.
- **Fly over (drone shot)** — the camera flies high above, like a bird.
- **Still shot** — the camera doesn't move at all.

## 1. Warm-up — Be the camera (10 min · 1-on-1: 5 min)
Use a phone camera. Film a toy or a mug for 5 seconds with each move: walk closer (zoom in), turn your body (pan), walk around it (circle around). Watch them back. Which one feels exciting? Which feels calm?

```group
Pairs film each other's objects. Share 2 favorites on the big screen.
```

```solo
The student films; you call out the moves like a movie director: "Action! Pan left! Now circle around!" Then swap. Watch them back together.
```

## 2. Mini-lesson — Moves and feelings (15 min · 1-on-1: 10 min)
Play your side-by-side clips (**Visual 1**):

| Move | What it does | Feeling | Prompt words |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🔍 Zoom in | Gets closer | "Look at this!" Focus, surprise | "the camera slowly zooms in on…" |
| ↔️ Pan | Turns left or right | Showing a place, a reveal | "the camera pans from left to right across…" |
| 🏃 Follow | Moves with the subject | Action, being part of it | "the camera follows behind the runner" |
| 🔄 Circle around | Goes around the subject | "This is special!" Products, heroes | "the camera circles around the bottle" |
| 🦅 Fly over | High up, moving | Big, wide, epic | "drone shot flying over the forest" |
| 📷 Still | Doesn't move | Calm, or lets the action speak | "still camera, the subject moves" |

**Tips for AI video:**
- **One move per clip.** Two moves at once often gets messy.
- Say the speed: *slowly*, *quickly*, *smoothly*.
- Product shots love **circle around** and **zoom in**. Opening shots love **fly over** and **pan**.

```solo
Play each clip without saying the move. Let the student name it first. Then ask: "Where would that fit in YOUR storyboard?"
```

## 3. Hands-on build — Shoot your storyboard (35 min · 1-on-1: 25 min)
1. Look at your storyboard. Pick a camera move for each box. Write it in the "Camera" note.
2. Turn 2 or 3 boxes into Gemini video prompts with all 6 formula parts. Use the **same style words** in every prompt so the shots match.
3. Make the clips. Save them named by box number: `box1-hook.mp4`, `box2-problem.mp4`.
4. Check each clip: did the camera do what you asked? Any glitches?

**Checkpoint 1 (+20 XP):** every storyboard box has a camera move.
**Checkpoint 2 (+20 XP):** 2 or more clips made and saved in order.

```group
Remind everyone: clips are limited. Plan the prompt before pressing Enter.
```

```solo
Start with the hook box. It matters most. If the clip isn't right, look at it together and change only one word before trying again.
```

## 4. Game / challenge — Guess the Move (15 min · 1-on-1: 10 min)
Play 6 short clips (or real movie trailers with the sound off). Name the camera move in each one, and the feeling it gives.

```group
Houses write answers on whiteboards. Most right: +15 XP. Everyone: +5.
```

```solo
**Student vs. Bolt.** Take turns: one person acts out a camera move with their hands and a phone, and the other names it in 5 seconds. Then watch 4 clips and race to name each move. +15 XP for playing, +5 for 4 or more right.
```

## 5. Share-out — Director's cut (10 min · 1-on-1: 5 min)
Show your clips in order. For one clip, say: "I used [move] because I wanted the viewer to feel [feeling]."

```solo
The student plays their clips and explains one move choice. You say which shot you'd keep for sure, and which one you'd redo.
```

## 6. Exit ticket (5 min)
1. Name 4 camera moves.
2. Which move makes a product look special? Why?
3. Why is one move per clip a good rule?

```solo
Ask out loud. For #1, they can act the moves out with their hands.
```

## Differentiation
- **Extra help:** use only 3 moves today: zoom in, pan and still. Give the exact prompt words to copy.
- **Extra challenge:** try a "reveal": start close on a detail, then pull back to show the whole scene. Can Gemini do it in one clip?
- **1-on-1 tip:** watch one minute of a favorite movie scene together with the sound off, and count the camera moves.
