## Lesson objectives
By the end, the student can…
- Look for clues that a video might be made by AI.
- Use Gemini to check if a picture or video has Google's hidden AI mark.
- Explain how **deepfakes** can hurt people, and what to do if you see one.
- Add an honest **AI label** to their own ad.

## Words for today
- **Deepfake** — a fake video, picture or voice of a real person, made with AI.
- **Hidden mark (SynthID)** — a mark you can't see that Google puts in pictures and videos made with its AI.
- **AI label** — words on the video like "Made with AI".
- **Source** — where something came from. Who posted it first?
- **Scam** — a trick to get your money or your private info.

## 1. Warm-up — Real or AI? (10 min · 1-on-1: 5 min)
Play 8 short clips. For each one: real or AI? Point to a clue. Then reveal. Ask: "Was it harder than with pictures? Why?"

```group
Houses vote on whiteboards. Keep score.
```

```solo
The student votes and gives a clue for each. Keep score. Can they beat 6 out of 8? Then let them make one clip in Gemini to try to fool YOU.
```

## 2. Mini-lesson — How fakes trick people (15 min · 1-on-1: 10 min)
**Clues that a video might be AI** (**Visual 1**). None of these is proof, but they're good reasons to check:
- Hands, teeth or eyes look odd. Faces "slide" a little.
- Words and signs wiggle or don't make sense.
- Things melt, pop in or vanish. Physics feels wrong.
- Lips don't quite match the voice.
- It's very short, very shocking, and has no clear source.

**Why deepfakes hurt** (**Visual 2**):
- **Scams:** a fake video of a famous person selling a "get rich" trick. A fake voice call asking for money.
- **Bullying:** putting someone's face into a mean fake video.
- **Lies spread fast:** fake news clips get shared before anyone checks.

**What to do:**
1. **Stop** before you share.
2. **Check the source:** who posted it first? Is it a real news site?
3. **Check for AI:** upload it to Gemini and ask "Was this made with Google AI?" It looks for Google's hidden mark. A "no" doesn't prove it's real (other apps don't add Google's mark), so keep checking.
4. **Tell someone** if it's hurting a real person. Report it on the app.

```teacher
The better AI video gets, the more our eyes get fooled. So we can't just trust our eyes. We trust checking: who posted it, can I find it somewhere else, and does it have an AI mark?
```

## 3. Hands-on build — Detective + honest label (30 min · 1-on-1: 20 min)
**Part A — Check 4 clips (15 min · 1-on-1: 10 min):** for 4 clips from the warm-up set, fill in:

| Clip | Clues I see | Gemini check | Source check | My answer |
|---|---|---|---|---|

**Checkpoint 1 (+20 XP):** 4 clips checked with all 3 checks.

**Part B — Label your ad (15 min · 1-on-1: 10 min):**
1. Add a clear label to your ad: "Made with AI" on the end card, or a small "AI" tag in a corner the whole time.
2. In the video's description, write which parts are AI (clips, voice, music) and which are yours (story, script, editing).

**Checkpoint 2 (+20 XP):** label added + "what's AI, what's mine" note written.

```solo
Do Part A side by side: the student runs the Gemini check, you do the source check, then swap. For Part B, ask: "Would a viewer feel tricked if they found out later?" If yes, the label isn't clear enough.
```

## 4. Game / challenge — Share or Stop? (20 min · 1-on-1: 15 min)
Read each card. **Share**, **Stop** or **Check first**? Give a reason.
1. A video of a famous singer saying "Send me $20 and I'll send you $200!" → Stop (scam)
2. Your friend's face put into a silly AI video, posted without asking → Stop. Tell them, and report it.
3. A shocking news clip with no source, shared 1 million times → Check first
4. Your own AI ad, with a "Made with AI" label → Share
5. A video of your principal saying school is cancelled, from an account you don't know → Check first (ask the school)
6. An AI video of a cute cartoon dog, labeled as AI → Share
7. A voice message that sounds like your cousin asking for your password → Stop (call them back on their real number)
8. An AI ad that shows a product doing something it can't really do → Stop (that's not honest)

```group
Houses get 2 minutes per card. Best reasons: +15 XP. Everyone who finishes: +5.
```

```solo
**Hot seat.** Read each card. The student answers in 10 seconds and gives a reason. After 4, swap: they read, you answer, and you get one wrong on purpose. Can they catch it? +15 XP for playing, +5 for catching you.
```

## 5. Share-out — The Honest Creator promise (10 min · 1-on-1: 5 min)
Add one line to your Safety Pact from Module 0: "When I make AI video, I promise to…" Read it out loud.

```solo
The student reads their promise to you. Then add the line to their pact for their portfolio.
```

## 6. Exit ticket (5 min)
1. Name 3 clues that a video might be AI.
2. What should you do before you share a shocking video?
3. Why does a "no" from the Gemini check not prove a video is real?

```solo
Ask out loud. For #3, help them say it simply: "Other apps don't add Google's mark."
```

## Differentiation
- **Extra help:** check 2 clips instead of 4. Use the clue card as a checklist.
- **Extra challenge:** find a real news story about a deepfake scam. Share what happened and how people could have spotted it, in 60 seconds (+25 XP side quest).
- **1-on-1 tip:** ask the student if they've ever seen a video they weren't sure was real. Start from their story.
