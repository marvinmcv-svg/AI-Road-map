## Lesson objectives
By the end, the student can…
- **Cut** and **trim** clips so every shot is only as long as it needs to be.
- Put shots in story order and line them up with the voiceover.
- Explain **pace**, and finish a **rough cut** of the ad.

## Words for today
- **Cut** — where one shot stops and the next one starts.
- **Trim** — chop off the boring start or end of a clip.
- **Timeline** — the strip at the bottom of the editor that shows all your clips in order.
- **Pace** — how fast the video feels. Short shots = fast. Long shots = slow.
- **Rough cut** — the first, messy version of your video. You'll make it better later.
- **Transition** — how one shot changes into the next (a plain cut is usually best).

## 1. Warm-up — Too slow! (10 min · 1-on-1: 5 min)
Play the "too slow" ad, then the real one. Ask: "Which felt boring? Where did you want to skip?" Then: "What did the editor cut out?"

```group
Everyone raises a hand the moment they get bored. Note when most hands go up.
```

```solo
The student says "boring!" out loud the moment they want to skip. Write down the seconds. Then watch the real one and compare.
```

## 2. Mini-lesson — 5 editing moves (12 min · 1-on-1: 8 min)
Show it live in **Google Vids** (**Visual 1**):
1. **Add clips** to the timeline in storyboard order.
2. **Trim** every clip. Cut the slow start and end. Most shots in a short ad last 1 to 3 seconds!
3. **Line up the voice.** Each line should play over the matching shot.
4. **Add music** under everything. Make it quieter than the voice.
5. **Plain cuts** most of the time. Fancy transitions (spins, swirls) look messy if you use too many.

**Pace tips:**
- Start fast. The hook should hit in the first 1 to 2 seconds.
- Cut on the action (in the middle of a jump or a splash). It feels smooth.
- End with the logo sting and the call to action on screen for 2 or 3 seconds.

Missing a shot? Make it in Gemini (or with Veo right inside Vids) and drop it in.

## 3. Hands-on build — Rough cut (43 min · 1-on-1: 30 min)
1. Start a new video in Google Vids. Add all your clips in storyboard order.
2. Trim each clip.
3. Add your voiceover. Line up each line with its shot.
4. Add music and the 2 sound effects you planned.
5. Watch it all the way through. Is it 30 to 60 seconds?
6. Make any missing shot in Gemini and add it.

**Checkpoint 1 (+20 XP):** all clips on the timeline, in order, trimmed.
**Checkpoint 2 (+20 XP):** voice, music and sound effects in. Rough cut plays from start to end.

```group
Walk around and ask: "What's the slowest shot? Can you trim it?"
```

```solo
Let the student drive the mouse. You only point and ask. When they get stuck, show it once, then hand the mouse back. Save often!
```

> 🛡️ **Music moment:** use music that comes with Google Vids or other free music made for videos. Don't use songs from the radio or streaming apps. They belong to the artists, and your video can get taken down.

## 4. Game / challenge — The Boring Meter (10 min · 1-on-1: 7 min)
Play your rough cut for someone. They hold a hand flat and raise it when they get bored. Note every second where the hand goes up. Trim those spots. Then play it again.

```group
Pairs swap and test each other. Lowest boring score (fewest hand raises): +15 XP. Everyone who fixes a spot: +5.
```

```solo
You are the boring meter first. Be honest! Then a guest if you have one. The student trims every boring spot and plays it again. +15 XP for playing, +5 for each spot fixed.
```

## 5. Share-out — First look (10 min · 1-on-1: 5 min)
Play the rough cut. Feedback: *I like… / I wonder… / Try…* Remember: it's a rough cut. It's supposed to be messy!

```solo
Write the student's own "fix list" together: the top 3 things to fix next lesson.
```

## 6. Exit ticket (5 min)
1. What's the difference between a cut and a trim?
2. What is pace?
3. Name one thing you'll fix in your rough cut.

```solo
Ask out loud. Keep their fix list at the top of next lesson's notes.
```

## Differentiation
- **Extra help:** a 20-second ad with 4 shots is fine. Give a checklist of the 5 editing moves to tick off.
- **Extra challenge:** make a tall 9:16 version for phones. What has to change? Add a beat-matched cut (cut right on the music's beat).
- **1-on-1 tip:** editing takes time. If the build runs long, skip the game and do the boring meter at the start of next lesson.
