## Lesson objectives
By the end, the student can…
- Finish a 30 to 60 second launch ad with a hook, captions, sound and an honest AI label.
- Score their own video with the rubric and fix the weakest part.
- Present it and explain 2 choices they made, using video words.

## Words for today
All the Module 2 words. Most of all: hook, storyboard, camera move, pace, captions, AI label.

---

## 1. Warm-up — Word lightning round (5 min)
Show a word. Say what it means and give an example. 10 words, fast.

```group
First house to answer scores.
```

```solo
Time it: how many words in 2 minutes? Try to beat the Module 1 score.
```

## 2. Mini-lesson — What "Pro" looks like (5 min)
Go through the rubric's **4 · Pro** column, one row at a time. Show a strong example (your own demo ad is great). Remind: the AI label and the "what's AI, what's mine" note are required.

## 3. Hands-on build — Final cut + self-check (35 min · 1-on-1: 25 min)
Final checklist:
- [ ] The hook lands in the first 3 seconds
- [ ] 4 beats: Hook → Problem → Fix → Call to action
- [ ] Shots are trimmed and the pace feels good (the boring meter is happy)
- [ ] Voice, music and sound effects are clear (music quieter than voice)
- [ ] Captions are big, short and spelled right, and it works on mute
- [ ] End card: logo + call to action
- [ ] AI label + "what's AI, what's mine" note
- [ ] Nothing untrue about the product, no real people, no copied music

Score yourself with the rubric. Write one sentence of proof for each row. Last 10 minutes: fix the lowest row. Then **export** (download) the video and save it in the folder.
**Checkpoint (+20 XP):** final video exported + self-check done.

```solo
Let the student self-score first, with no help. Then compare with your score. Where you disagree, watch that part again together.
```

## 4. Game / challenge — The Premiere (30 min · 1-on-1: 15 min)
Dim the lights. Show the title slide. Play the ad (and the explainer). Then the maker answers:
- "My hook is ___ because ___."
- "My favorite shot is ___. I used a ___ camera move to make people feel ___."
- "The AI made ___. I made ___."

Listeners fill in a score card (one ⭐ strength, one ❓ question). The teacher scores with the rubric.

**Boss XP:** rubric total (4 rows × 1 to 4 points) × 6 = up to 96. Round it up to 100 for a perfect score. **🎬 Hook Master** badge for 3 or more on every row.

```group
Awards: "Best Hook", "Best Sound", "Best Camera Move" (+15 house XP each).
```

```solo
**Red carpet.** Make it feel special: title slide, lights down, guests ready. Play it twice: once just to enjoy, once to talk about. Each guest asks one question. Award "Best in Show" to their best moment (+15 XP).
```

## 5. Share-out — Wrap party (10 min · 1-on-1: 5 min)
Play a mix of everyone's best 3-second hooks back to back. Then tease the next quest: *"Your ad is ready… but where will people go when they click? Your brand needs a WEBSITE."*

```solo
Help the student share the video with their family (only if they want to). Ask them to tell you the best part of the module.
```

## 6. Exit ticket (5 min)
1. What part of your video are you most proud of? Why?
2. What video skill will you use again on the website?
3. What would you change if you had one more lesson?

```solo
Ask out loud. Save the answers for a progress note.
```

## Differentiation
- **Extra help:** a 30-second ad is enough. The explainer is optional. Use the checklist as a step-by-step list.
- **Extra challenge:** make 3 versions for 3 places: wide 16:9 for a website, tall 9:16 for phones, and a 6-second "bumper" version.
- **1-on-1 tip:** a real audience matters. Even one guest on a video call makes the premiere feel big.
