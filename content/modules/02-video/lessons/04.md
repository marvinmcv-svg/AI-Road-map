## Lesson objectives
By the end, the student can…
- Turn a still picture into a moving clip in Gemini (**photo to video**).
- Keep a character looking the same in several clips with a **start picture** and a fixed description.
- Tell Gemini only what should MOVE, not what the picture already shows.

## Words for today
- **Photo to video** — you give the AI a picture, and it makes that picture move.
- **Start picture (start frame)** — the picture the clip begins with.
- **Same every time (consistency)** — the character looks like the same one in every clip.
- **Character sheet** (again) — the exact words that describe your character.
- **Loop** — a short clip that plays again and again, like a moving sticker.

## 1. Warm-up — Recall Raid (10 min · 1-on-1: 5 min)
```teacher
Today your pictures come to life! You will turn a still picture into a moving video, and keep your character the same. Quick quiz first.
```

1. What are 2 tricks for keeping a character the same in pictures?
2. Name the 4 beats of a short ad.
3. Which camera move makes a product look special?
4. What's a hook?
5. Why do clips sometimes have glitches?

```solo
Rapid fire, out loud. Then the student opens their mascot picture from Module 1 and tells you its name and personality again.
```

## 2. Mini-lesson — From still to moving (15 min · 1-on-1: 10 min)
Show your demo (**Visual 1**):
- **Words only:** "a small round teal robot waves" → the robot looks a bit different every time.
- **Picture + words:** upload the mascot picture, then: "Make this robot wave hello and smile. The camera stays still." → it looks the same as the picture!

**How to write a photo-to-video prompt:**
1. The picture already shows WHO and WHAT. Don't describe it all again.
2. Describe the **motion**: what moves, how, and how fast.
3. Add the **camera** and the **sound**.
4. Keep it small. One action is best: wave, jump, spin, pour, open.

*Example:* "Make this water bottle spin slowly as cold drops slide down the side. The camera slowly circles around it. Soft fizzy sound and calm music."

**For a whole video with the same character:**
- Make a **start picture** for each storyboard box in Gemini first, using the character sheet. Then turn each picture into a clip.
- Same style words, same light, same colors every time.

## 3. Hands-on build — Make it move (35 min · 1-on-1: 25 min)
1. **Mascot clip:** upload your mascot picture. Make it do one action that fits your brand.
2. **Product clip:** upload your product picture. Make it spin, open, pour or splash.
3. **Logo sting:** upload your logo. Make it appear in a fun way (pop in, draw itself, sparkle) with a sound. This is the end of your ad!
4. Check each clip for changes: does the mascot keep the same colors, eyes and shape?

**Checkpoint 1 (+20 XP):** mascot clip + product clip, mascot looks the same as the picture.
**Checkpoint 2 (+20 XP):** logo sting saved, plus one storyboard box remade as photo-to-video.

```group
Pairs check each other's clips for changes with the "Same or Changed?" list.
```

```solo
Do the first upload together so the student sees where the button is. Then let them run the rest. Pause each clip on the last frame and compare it with the picture side by side.
```

> 🛡️ **Asking first moment:** photo to video works on real photos too, so this is a powerful tool. We only animate our OWN things: our mascot, our product, our logo, and photos of objects. Never a real person's photo without their clear "yes", and never to make them do or say something they didn't.

## 4. Game / challenge — Spot the Change (15 min · 1-on-1: 10 min)
Play clips where the character changes a little (a color, an eye, a hat that disappears). Pause on the first and last frames. Find every change.

```group
Houses circle changes on printouts of the first and last frames. Most found: +15 XP. Everyone: +5.
```

```solo
**Student vs. Bolt.** Each of you makes one clip of the mascot and secretly tries to make it change as little as possible. Swap and hunt for changes. Fewest changes wins. +15 XP for playing, +5 for the win.
```

## 5. Share-out — Moving sticker show (10 min · 1-on-1: 5 min)
Play the logo sting and the mascot clip. Ask: "Does it still feel like OUR brand?"

```solo
The student shows their best clip to you (and a guest if you have one). You say one thing that moved really well, and one thing that changed that shouldn't have.
```

## 6. Exit ticket (5 min)
1. What does "photo to video" mean?
2. In a photo-to-video prompt, what should you describe most?
3. Why do we only animate our own pictures and objects?

```solo
Ask out loud. Then save all of today's clips in the folder in storyboard order.
```

## Differentiation
- **Extra help:** start with the logo sting only (easiest), then the product clip.
- **Extra challenge:** make a 3-clip mini story where the mascot stays the same in all 3. Or make a perfect loop that could be a moving sticker.
- **1-on-1 tip:** if the student has a pet or a favorite toy, a photo of it is a great practice picture (objects and animals only, no people).
