## 🔥 Warm-up: Real or AI?
Watch 8 short clips. Real or AI? Point to a clue!

## 💡 Big idea: stop, check, tell
**Clues a video might be AI** (clues mean "check it", not "it's fake for sure"):
- ✋ odd hands, teeth or eyes
- 🔤 wiggly words and signs
- 🫠 things melt, pop in or vanish
- 👄 lips don't match the voice
- ❓ very shocking, with no clear source

**Before you share:**
1. 🛑 **Stop**
2. 🔎 **Check** who posted it first, and upload it to Gemini: "Was this made with Google AI?" (A "no" doesn't prove it's real. Other apps don't add Google's mark.)
3. 🗣️ **Tell** someone if it hurts a real person, and report it

## 🛡️ Stay safe
A **deepfake** puts a real person's face or voice into a fake video. It can be used for scams and bullying. Never make one, and never share one.
