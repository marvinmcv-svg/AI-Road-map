## 🔥 Warm-up: Too slow!
Watch a slow version of an ad. Say "boring!" the moment you want to skip. What would you cut?

## 💡 Big idea: 5 editing moves
1. ➕ **Add clips** to the timeline in storyboard order
2. ✂️ **Trim** the slow start and end of each clip (most shots: 1 to 3 seconds!)
3. 🗣️ **Line up the voice** with the right shot
4. 🎵 **Add music** under everything, quieter than the voice
5. 🎬 **Use plain cuts** most of the time. Too many fancy spins look messy.

**Pace tips:** start fast · cut in the middle of the action · end with the logo and call to action for 2 or 3 seconds.
