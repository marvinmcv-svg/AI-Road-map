## 💡 Big idea
A video is lots of pictures shown fast. Each one is a **frame**. Most videos show about 24 frames every second. AI video has to keep every frame matching, so the dog doesn't turn into a cat halfway! Gemini's video maker (**Veo**) makes clips about 8 seconds long, with sound.

**The Video Prompt Formula**
| Part | Ask yourself | Try words like |
|---|---|---|
| 🎬 Subject & action | Who or what? Doing what? | a robot waters a plant |
| 📍 Setting | Where? When? | sunny windowsill, morning |
| 🎨 Style | What look? | 3D cartoon, real-life video, clay |
| 🎥 Camera | How close? Does it move? | close-up, slowly zooms in |
| ☀️ Light & mood | How should it feel? | warm and calm, bright and fun |
| 🔊 Sound | What do we hear? | birds, soft piano, a happy beep |

```try
A small round robot waters a tiny plant on a sunny windowsill in the morning, 3D cartoon, close-up, the camera slowly zooms in, warm and calm, soft piano music and a happy beep, 16:9.
```
