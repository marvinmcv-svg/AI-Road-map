## 🔥 Warm-up: Be the camera
Film a toy with a phone. Walk closer. Turn your body. Walk around it. Which one feels exciting?

## 💡 Big idea: camera moves
| Move | Feeling | Prompt words |
|---|---|---|
| 🔍 Zoom in | "Look at this!" | the camera slowly zooms in on… |
| ↔️ Pan | showing a place | the camera pans left to right across… |
| 🏃 Follow | action, you're in it | the camera follows behind… |
| 🔄 Circle around | "So special!" | the camera circles around… |
| 🦅 Fly over | big and epic | drone shot flying over… |
| 📷 Still | calm | still camera, the subject moves |

**Tip:** one move per clip. Say the speed: slowly, quickly, smoothly.

```try
A bright blue water bottle on a mossy rock by a waterfall, real-life video style, the camera slowly circles around the bottle, sunny and fresh, the sound of rushing water, 16:9.
```

## 🛠️ Your build: Shoot your storyboard
- [ ] Pick a camera move for every storyboard box
- [ ] Write 6-part prompts for 2 or 3 boxes (same style words every time)
- [ ] Make the clips in Gemini
- [ ] Save them named by box number (box1-hook, box2-problem…)
- [ ] Check: did the camera do what you asked? Any glitches?

## ⚔️ Challenge: Guess the Move
Watch clips and name the camera move. How does it make you feel?

```group
Your house writes answers on a whiteboard.
```

```solo
**You vs. your teacher.** Take turns acting out a move with a phone. The other person names it in 5 seconds!
```

## 🎟️ Exit ticket
1. Name 4 camera moves.
2. Which move makes a product look special?
3. Why is one move per clip a good rule?

## 🛡️ Stay safe
When you film for real, film things and places. No one else without their "yes".

## 🚀 Bonus quest (+25 XP)
