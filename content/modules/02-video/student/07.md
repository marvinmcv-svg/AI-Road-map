## 🔥 Warm-up: Mute test
Watch your ad with the sound off. Can you still tell what it's about?

## 💡 Big idea: words that work
Lots of people watch videos on mute. Captions also help people who can't hear well.
- 🔠 **Big** — easy to read on a phone
- ✂️ **Short** — 3 to 7 words at a time
- ⏱️ **Long enough** — so you can read them twice
- 🌗 **Contrast** — white words with a dark edge
- 📱 **Safe zone** — keep words in the middle, away from phone buttons
- ✅ **Spelled right** — check every word

**Explainer video:** Title card → Step 1 → Step 2 → Step 3 → End card with logo.
