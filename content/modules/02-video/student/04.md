## 🔥 Warm-up: Recall Raid
5 quick questions from Module 1 and this module. How many can you get?

## 💡 Big idea: photo to video
Upload a picture to Gemini and ask it to move. The picture already shows WHO and WHAT. So your prompt should say:
1. 🏃 **What moves**, and how fast
2. 🎥 **The camera**
3. 🔊 **The sound**

Keep it to one action: wave, jump, spin, pour, open.
Your mascot will look much more the same than with words only!

```try
Make this water bottle spin slowly as cold drops slide down the side. The camera slowly circles around it. Soft fizzy sound and calm music.
```

Look at the first and last frames of a clip. Find everything that changed!

```group

```

```solo
**You vs. your teacher.** You each make a mascot clip that changes as little as possible. Swap and hunt for changes!
```

## 🎟️ Exit ticket
1. What does "photo to video" mean?
2. In a photo-to-video prompt, what should you describe most?
3. Why do we only animate our own pictures and objects?

## 🛡️ Stay safe
Photo to video works on real photos of people too. That's why our rule is: only our own things, objects and made-up characters. Never a real person without their clear "yes".
