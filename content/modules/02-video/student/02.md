## 🔥 Warm-up: Stop or scroll?
Watch the first 3 seconds of 4 ads. Would you keep watching, or scroll away? Why?

## 💡 Big idea: the 4-part ad
| Beat | When | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 🪝 **Hook** | 0–3 seconds | Something surprising, funny or curious |
| 😩 **Problem** | next | The buyer's problem |
| ✨ **Fix** | middle | Your product saves the day |
| 👉 **Call to action** | end | What to do now + your logo |

**5 kinds of hooks:** a question · a surprise · a big sound · "POV:" · before and after.

A **storyboard** is your video as a comic strip. Plan it BEFORE you make clips!

## 🛠️ Your build: Storyboard
- [ ] Ask Gemini for 5 hook ideas for your brand. Pick one.
- [ ] Write one sentence for each of the 4 beats
- [ ] Make 4 to 6 storyboard boxes in Google Slides
- [ ] Make a Gemini picture for each box (same style every time)
- [ ] Under each box write: Action · Camera · Sound · Words on screen

```try
Give me 5 hook ideas for a 30-second ad for a water bottle that never leaks, for kids who love sports. Make them short and surprising.
```

## ⚔️ Challenge: Hook Factory
Pick a boring thing (socks, a pencil). Write as many hooks as you can in 3 minutes!

```group
Your house writes hooks together. The class votes for the best.
```

```solo
**You vs. your teacher.** You both write hooks. Read them back and forth. Pick each other's best one!
```

## 🎟️ Exit ticket
1. What are the 4 beats of a short ad?
2. Write 2 different hooks for your brand.
3. Why make a storyboard before making clips?

## 🛡️ Stay safe
The "Fix" part must be true. No fake results!
