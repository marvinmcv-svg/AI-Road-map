## 🔥 Warm-up: Word Lightning Round
Your teacher shows a word. Say what it means and give an example. Fast!

## 💡 What "Pro" looks like
Look at the rubric with your teacher. Your video needs an **AI label** and a **"what's AI, what's mine"** note.

## 🛠️ Your build: Final cut
- [ ] The hook lands in the first 3 seconds
- [ ] 4 beats: Hook → Problem → Fix → Call to action
- [ ] Shots are trimmed and the pace feels good
- [ ] Voice, music and sound effects are clear
- [ ] Captions are big, short, spelled right, and it works on mute
- [ ] End card: logo + call to action
- [ ] AI label + "what's AI, what's mine" note
- [ ] Nothing untrue, no real people, no copied music
- [ ] Score yourself with the rubric and fix your lowest row
- [ ] Export (download) the final video

## ⚔️ Challenge: The Premiere
Lights down! Play your ad. Then tell everyone:
> "My hook is ___ because ___. My favorite shot is ___. I used a ___ camera move to make people feel ___. The AI made ___. I made ___."

```group
Give every maker one ⭐ strength and one ❓ question on your score card.
```

```solo
Show it to your teacher and a guest. Watch it once to enjoy, then once to talk about it. Each guest asks you one question!
```

## 🎟️ Exit ticket
1. What part of your video are you most proud of? Why?
2. What video skill will you use again on the website?
3. What would you change with one more lesson?

## 🛡️ Stay safe
Only share your video if you want to. Keep the AI label on it wherever it goes.
