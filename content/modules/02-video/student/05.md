## 🔥 Warm-up: Same clip, new feeling
Watch one ad 3 times: with its sound, with no sound, and with silly music. How did the feeling change?

## 💡 Big idea: the 3 kinds of sound
- 🗣️ **Voice** — tells the story. Short sentences. Smile when you talk!
- 🎵 **Music** — sets the mood. Keep it quieter than the voice.
- 💥 **Sound effects** — make actions feel real: a splash, a click, a whoosh.

**Timing rule:** people say about 2 to 3 words a second. A 30-second ad fits about **65 words**.

```try
Write a 30-second voiceover for my ad. Here are my 4 story beats: [paste them]. Use about 65 words, short sentences, fun and friendly, for kids who love sports. Show which storyboard box each line goes with.
```

## 🛠️ Your build: Write it, voice it
- [ ] Get a script from Gemini, then change it so it sounds like YOU
- [ ] Read it out loud with a timer. Too long? Cut words.
- [ ] Record it in Google Vids (your voice or an AI voice)
- [ ] Do 3 takes and pick the best one
- [ ] Pick background music that fits your brand
- [ ] Plan 2 sound effects and where they go

## ⚔️ Challenge: Sound Match
Watch silent clips. Pick (or make!) the best music and sound effects for each one.

```group
Your house makes the sound effects live while the clip plays!
```

```solo
**Live foley!** Make sound effects with your voice and things on the desk while the clip plays. Then your teacher tries!
```

## 🎟️ Exit ticket
1. Name the 3 kinds of video sound.
2. About how many words fit in a 30-second ad?
3. Why do we never copy a real person's voice?

## 🛡️ Stay safe
Use your own voice or a preset AI voice. Never a copy of someone else's voice. Fake voices are used in scams.

## 🚀 Bonus quest (+25 XP)
